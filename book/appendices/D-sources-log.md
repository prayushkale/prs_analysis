# Appendix D: Source Citation Notes

This book is compiled from 3,253 transcripts of daily market commentary and educational videos (2018–2026). Market events cited in the chronicle and case studies were verified against NSE India, SEBI circulars, RBI press releases, PIB, Reuters, Economic Times, Moneycontrol, Business Standard, and Yahoo Finance. Direct event-level sources are recorded in `book/events/events_verified.jsonl`.

Glossary terms draw from NSE definitions and standard derivatives literature.
