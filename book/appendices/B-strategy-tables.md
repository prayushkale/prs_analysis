# Appendix B: Strategy Quick-Reference

| Strategy | Construction | Best Regime | Max Risk |
|----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Short Straddle | Sell ATM Call + Sell ATM Put | Low VIX, range-bound market | Unlimited (both sides) |
| Short Strangle | Sell OTM Call + Sell OTM Put | Low VIX, expected range holds | Unlimited (both sides) |
| Bull Call Spread | Buy ATM Call + Sell OTM Call | Bullish, capped upside | Premium paid |
| Bear Put Spread | Buy ATM Put + Sell OTM Put | Bearish, capped downside | Premium paid |
| Call Calendar | Sell near-month Call + Buy far-month Call | Consolidation after big move | Time decay loss on far leg |
| Put Calendar | Sell near-month Put + Buy far-month Put | Consolidation after big move | Time decay loss on far leg |
| Call Ratio Spread | Buy 1 OTM Call + Sell 2+ further OTM Calls | Mildly bullish, downside neutral | Unlimited on extreme rally |
| Put Ratio Spread | Buy 1 OTM Put + Sell 2+ further OTM Puts | Mildly bearish, upside neutral | Unlimited on extreme fall |
| Call Butterfly | Buy 1 lower strike + Sell 2 middle + Buy 1 higher strike | Range-bound after earnings/events | Premium paid (limited) |
| Put Butterfly | Same construction in puts | Range-bound, bearish bias | Premium paid (limited) |
| BeES + Protective Put | Buy Nifty BeES + Buy far-dated OTM Put | Long-term bullish with tail risk | Premium of put + exit at support |
| Covered Call | Own 100 shares + Sell 1 OTM Call | Long stock, mild upside expected | Stock holds downside uncapped |
| Cash-Secured Put | Assign cash + Sell 1 OTM Put at strike you'd buy | Want to buy stock at discount | Stock falls below strike |
