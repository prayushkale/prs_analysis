Do not trade in stock options. Trade only in index options. If you are a low risk taker, trade only in nifty options. Of course, if you are a high risk taker, you can trade in bank nifty options also. Indices cannot fall 50 percent, 60 percent in one day. In fact, if nifty falls 20 percent in a day, the exchange will have to be closed. So, relatively the index are more stable than the stocks. Just imagine if somebody has a long position in DHFL. If the stock falls in one day 65 percent, what will be the damage? So, the risk is really very high in stock options. So, therefore, I suggest you to trade in index options.
