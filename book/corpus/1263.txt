Let us say, I have a very big shopping complex and if I want to sell it, then the price of the shopping complex is 1000 crore. How many people can buy for 1000 crore? And because of that, the people who are ready to buy, they may be quoting a very very low price, there will be illiquidity in the market. So, what I can do, instead of selling my shopping complex to one person at 1000 crore and what I do, I just make 100 shares of 10 rupees each, total is 1000 crore. I just, the people who buy these shares, they become shareholder of this shopping complex. But in share market, it is the companies that are giving the shares. But here in this case, it is not a company, it is just a shopping complex. Here, we don't use the word share, we use the word real estate investment trust, which is called REIT.
