I think in 2004, the then finance minister, P. Chidambaram, there was a demand to abolish the long-term capital gain tax. So, he abolished the long-term capital gain tax. So, he then introduced this securities transaction tax in lieu of long-term capital gain tax. Now, what has happened when the BJP rule came, they brought back the long-term capital gain tax. So, STT was introduced in lieu of long-term capital gain tax. So, logically speaking, when the long-term capital gain tax is reintroduced, STD should go away. You are paying STT and you are not allowed to do the deduction on tax and then you are also paying the long-term capital gain tax. So, it becomes a double taxation. At least, please change the name from the securities transaction tax to security transaction punishment.
