I repeat few of the sayings in stock market, buy on rumour, sell on news, good news is a good news, very good news is a bad news, bad news is a bad news, very bad news is a good news. Markets worry about lead indicator not the lag indicator and most important thing there is something called efficient market hypothesis which says that you are the last one to know the fact. Whatever news is there in the public domain they are all already factored into the market. So therefore unless you have some insider information you cannot capitalize or you cannot make profit out of the information that you have because that information is known to everybody.
