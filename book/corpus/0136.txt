An American Depository Recept is a negotiable certificate issued by a US depository bank representing a specified number of shares, often one share, but it is not always one share of a foreign company stock. The ADR trades on US stock markets as any domestic shares would. ADRs offer US investors a way to purchase stock in overseas companies that would not be available otherwise. Foreign firms also benefit as ADRs enable them to attract American investors and capital without hassle and expense of listing on US stock exchanges. So key takeaways, an American Depository Recept is a certificate issued by US bank that represents shares of a foreign stock. ADRs trade on American stock exchanges. ADRs and their dividends are priced in US dollars. ADRs represent an easy liquid way for US investors to own foreign stocks. Okay, forget about the definition.
