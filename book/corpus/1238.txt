In futures, the buyer also pay the margin money and seller also pay the margin money. So if the markets go higher, the buyer makes profit and seller loses. The loss is called MTM loss and the profit is called MTM profit. So the person who is losing the money, he has to pay the money immediately to the exchange and exchange will return that money to the winning person. So let us say I am a buyer in future, you are seller in future and tomorrow you lose, you have to pay money, I will receive that money. The next day you gain money, then I lose it, so I have to pay money, you will receive money. So this is called marked market settlement every day. So in case of futures, both buyer and seller may gain or may lose on a day to day basis. So this is in case of futures.
