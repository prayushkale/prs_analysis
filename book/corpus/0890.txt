There is no such thing called safe strategy and second thing there is no such thing called fundamentally strong company. A company that may be fundamentally strong now may not be fundamentally strong after few years. In fact, once upon a time, Reliance Communication, Reliance Infrastructure, Reliance Capital, they were all fundamentally good companies. But what happened now? They are not fundamentally good. And no strategy in stock market will be a safe strategy. If that is the case, everybody will be making money by doing that safe strategy. So every strategy has its own risk and own reward. So you understand the risk and you understand the reward. So that is the best way to trade.
