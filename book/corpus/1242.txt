One man went to a church father and asked for a job. The father says no and he came out and he was thirsty. There were no shops near the church or for a quite considerable distance. He thought why not I set up a shop here and then he set up a small shop selling some snacks, juices like that. And later he has become one of the world famous retail chain. So that chain is called 7-11. In your life sometimes unplanned success will be coming, will be much more powerful than the planned success.
