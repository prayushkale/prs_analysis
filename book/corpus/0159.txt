Dollar index is just an index that shows the relative strength of the dollar, the relative strength of the US dollar against a basket of currencies. It is a basket of six currencies, Euro, Japanese, pound sterling, Canadian dollar, Swedish krona and Swiss franc. These are the countries which are doing the maximum trading with US and the percentage percentage weightage is also based on the trading volume with these countries. Euro has 57.6 percentage weight, Japanese yen has 13.6 percentage weight, pound sterling 11.9, Canadian dollar 9.1, Swedish krona 4.2, Swiss franc 3.6. This index was started in 1973 but during the last 50 years the changes have been made only once. Earlier it was a German currency which was in the index and later that was replaced by Euro because since 1973 there was no Euro.
