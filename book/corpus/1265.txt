You can keep a stop-loss for positional trade also, but in India it is not possible. Say for example, you are buying Reliance Industries at 1750 and you want to keep a stop-loss at 1650. You can place the stop-loss in the trading system. There is a provision called Good to Cancel. So if you choose that, until you cancel that stop-loss order, that stop-loss order will continue to be in the system every day. But in India, we don't have such facilities, you have to put the stop-loss every day in the trading system. However, some brokers are trying to bring that facility in a different way. You put the stop-loss in the system, if you do not cancel it, by 3.30, it automatically gets cancelled by the exchange, but your broker terminal will remember it and will place the same order next day morning on your behove.
