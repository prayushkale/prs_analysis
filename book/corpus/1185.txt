One housewife was negotiating with a vegetable vendor. The vegetable vendor was selling a green leaf. How much is the green leaf? She was saying 10 rupees. The housewife saying, no, no, it should be 8 rupees only. And then suddenly that vegetable vendor felt very dizzy. Then the housewife ordered her son to bring some breakfast and some coffee to her. And then that vegetable vendor after eating, she went away. The son asked the mother. Mother, you are negotiating for 2 rupees. But you gave her the breakfast, you gave her the coffee that is more than 100 rupees. Then the mother said, my dear son, don't mix business with charity and don't mix charity with the business. So what I was negotiating for the green leaf, that is a business. What I offered her the breakfast and coffee, that is a charity.
