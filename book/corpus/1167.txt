Once upon a time, in Delhi, there was lot of snakes, it was a big problem. The government announced, whoever is killing the snake and bringing it to us, and we will pay you some money. And what has happened, after few months, when some government officials raided some houses, in many houses, people were growing the snake. So, people grow snakes, multiple snakes, so that they can bring and give it to the government. So, these two are the examples to show, how sometimes government does something with a good intention, but it is not being transpired with the right effect.
