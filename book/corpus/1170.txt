Stock market is not for the people who are expecting a regular return or a fixed return. I always used to tell that stock market is like a god and whatever the god gives we have to be happy. Let me tell you how the real HNIs play in the market. The real HNIs put their money in debt mutual funds or bank FD and they give that as a collateral to the exchange. So they are happy with the primary source of income from debt funds or FD and then whatever the money that they are getting from the stock market is an additional return. So when the additional return comes 10 percent or 20 percent or 30 percent whatever it comes we have to be happy. So this should be the right mindset of the option seller.
