Once there were three people one American, one Russian and one Indian. They came together in a party and they wanted to show their wealth and what this US guy did he took a hundred dollar note put some tobacco into the hundred dollars note and then rolled it and he started smoking just to show off his wealth. Then Russian has seen this and he took a thousand dollar note and he put some tobacco and rolled it and he started smoking just to show he is more wealthier than American. Then Indian has seen that what he has done he took a checkbook and took a check leaf and he has written one million dollar and then put some tobacco on the check leaf rolled the check leaf and started smoking. This is a joke how Indians show off without spending anything.
