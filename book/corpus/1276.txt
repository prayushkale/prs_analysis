I can show in NSC website where you can see what instrument you can give as a collateral. So if you go to nscindia.com, you go to products, equity derivatives, current market reports, you can see haircut for approved securities. If you click on this file, one excel file will be downloaded. So there it can tell you what are the shares you can give as a collateral and what is a haircut percentage. And not only that, if you click view all daily reports, just before haircut for approved securities, you can also see the haircut for mutual funds. The biggest advantage for option seller in India is that they can give equity shares, bonds, mutual funds and fixed deposit as collateral to the exchange and still they can do selling of options and of course profit and loss is different.
