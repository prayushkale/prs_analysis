People say real estate is the best form of investment and actually that is not true. In fact, the best example I can give you is Mumbai Nariman Point. The property prices have not appreciated for many decades. That is because most of the corporates shifted their base from Nariman Point to BKC. The same way in Chennai, there is a place called Mount Road where the property prices has not appreciated for the last 12 years. In fact, it has depreciated. So remember, real estate is for your personal consumption, not for investment.
