First, let us try to understand the meaning of the word Derivative. So, derivative means something derived from the underlying. Let me give you one real life example. So, you have got milk. From the milk, the curd comes. So, therefore, curd is a derivative of milk. Here, milk is the underlying and the curd is the derivative. Then, you add some water. Then, that becomes a buttermilk. So, curd is an underlying, buttermilk is a derivative. Then, from the buttermilk, you are taking the butter out. So, butter is derived from buttermilk. Buttermilk is derived from curd. Curd is derived from milk. So, these are the real life examples of derivatives.
