Do you know some good quality stocks that people call them as blue chip stocks? Have you ever thought why it is blue chip? Why not red chip or orange chip? Yes, there is a reason. If you go to a casino for gambling, you cannot play with the currency. So there will be a counter. You have to go and give the money to them and they will give you plastic chips instead of currency. So you have to use only those plastic chips for playing. Just like you have 5 rupee note, 10 rupee note, the chips that they give also will have values. Among all the chips, the blue color chip is the one having the highest value in a casino. So that is why the stocks with highest value are called blue chip stocks.
