You cannot buy after 3.30, but however, before 9.15, you can buy. So there is something called pre-market that operates from 9 a.m. to 9.07. During this period, you want to buy shares, you can buy, you can place the buy order, but you cannot buy. You want to sell, you can place the sell order, but it will not get executed. If the order matching is done after the end of pre-market and then at 9.15, the orders are getting matched and triggered.
