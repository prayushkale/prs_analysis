The trading or investing may not be as easy as you are thinking. Sometimes stocks can go in negative territory even for decades. In 1989, Japanese index called Nikkei was trading around 40,000 and after that it crashed. It has never recovered for the next 10 years. And after 10 years the Japanese people they felt that in their life they have lost 10 years. They coined a new word called last decade. That means one decade has been lost. But to their surprise even after 30 years their market still not recovered. In 1989 it was 40,000 now it is only about 20,000 plus.
