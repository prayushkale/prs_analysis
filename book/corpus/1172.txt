In simple language, the gamma effect is a violent move towards the end of the expiry. Today morning I saw Bank Nifty 38000 call option was trading around 10 rupees, it has shot up to 100 rupees. That's because you know because of short covering Bank Nifty shot up 1000 rupees today. So 10 rupees option can go to 100 rupees, if you are a seller you will lose heavily. This kind of violent move towards the end of the expiry is called gamma effect. Why? Because if this happens in the beginning of the expiry, the option seller don't lose so much. In the beginning of the expiry, the option may be trading at 100 rupees, it will not go to 1000 rupees. But closer to the expiry, 10 rupees option can go to 100 rupees, even 500 rupees.
