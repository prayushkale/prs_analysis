I'll give you an interesting aspect of psychology. A buyer, let us say he buy the option for 50 rupees, it goes to 100 rupees. So, he has already doubled his money. But what he will do? Will he book the profit? If he book the profit, then it will go to 200. So, where to book the profit? That is a very big dilemma for option buyers. And they don't book the profit at 100 rupees, then it goes to zero. You must have seen, especially on expiry days, option from 10 rupees will go to 50 rupees, then from 50 rupees it will go to zero rupees. So, though option buyer carries an unlimited reward, that affects his psychology, where to book the profit. Whereas, for option seller, though his profit is limited, and he knows that is his maximum profit, so the greed never comes into play. So, either his stop-loss hit or his target is hit. So, that's it.
