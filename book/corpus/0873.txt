The STT was introduced long back, so it was 0.17% and then markets were not happy, so based on certain recommendations it was reduced to 0.1. After some time you have increased the STT from 0.1% to 0.5% to option traders, 90% of the volume in Indian stock market happens through options. Trading in futures, you have no futures. Trading in options, you have plenty of options and people trading in futures, they have a huge risk. We educate the people to trade through options where the risk is comparatively less and because they choose to take lower risk and they are being punished with higher STT, it was 5 times more than the earlier STT.
