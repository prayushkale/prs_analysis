A forward is a contract between two parties and the contracts will be executed in a future date. But the price will be decided today and futures contract is almost same as forward contract but there is only one difference the price will not be decided today. You are a student going to US for studies and I am a businessman making mobile phones and selling in US. Your father can give you only Rs.75,000 you need $1000 every month and I need to sell my mobile phone at Rs.75 per dollar. So we decide the price 1 US dollar is 75 but for next 2 years I supply the dollar to you, you give me the Indian rupee but the price of Rs.75 is decided today that is forward. And future is exactly the same but the price is not decided today.
