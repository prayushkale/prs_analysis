Do you know what they call in U.S. expiry day? So, expiry day is known as witching day. Four times in a year, stock options, stock futures, index options, index futures, all the four will expire on the same day. That is why it is known as quadruple witching day. Quadruple means four. Even managing one witch is so difficult, it's a quadruple witching day. So, four witches come together. So, the markets tend to be hugely volatile.
