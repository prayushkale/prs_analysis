## Core lessons
1. In a falling-VIX environment, correct directional views still lose money - vega swamps delta for option buyers.
2. Weekly, monthly and far-month puts ALL fell or stagnated during a 500-point drop - the effect is structural, not decay noise.
3. Do not buy naked options when VIX is falling; run structures that own a short premium leg.
4. Television-expert option-buying advice during high VIX is exactly the trap to avoid.
5. Structure pre-earnings trades around the stock's one-month high/low band
6. Buying 1 and selling 2 at a higher strike creates a near-zero-cost position with capped downside
7. Far-out risk (above 410) is acceptable only when the stock has shown no ability to get there
8. Success needs two things: an edge (view/skill) and a hedge.
