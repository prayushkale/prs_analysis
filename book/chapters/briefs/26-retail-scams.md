## Core lessons
1. Trust only calls published before the level triggers; 'I said this morning in my private group' posts are retrofit scams.
2. India runs European-style options; early-exercise assignment would make hedged books unmanageable overnight.
3. Always square off before expiry: stock options and futures devolve into physical delivery.
4. Stock lending & borrowing survives mainly as a delivery-failure backstop because stock futures replaced shorting-in-cash.
5. Edge cannot be taught - it is the experience of reading the same news differently; hedge can.
6. Option selling is a book of many trades: a few failures drown in the aggregate profit - the insurance-pool principle.
7. Adjustments and firefighting (not stop-losses) are how a losing view becomes a small profit or small loss.
8. Personal P&L screenshots are marketing, not evidence: audit capital trajectory and consistency, not single screenshots (inversion of the scam test).

## Strategies
- **Sell-half-keep-half IPO strategy**: Apply minimum at cutoff via ASBA; on listing at 2x, sell 50% to recover principal
- **Hedged monthly book through expiry stress**: Directional futures/option legs + offsetting call hedges + occasional ratio spreads
- **High-beta stock short straddle with strike ladder**: 1,540 short straddle, then 1,520 and ~1,530 straddles added as price fell; puts 1,400 strike in second account; 10 lots
