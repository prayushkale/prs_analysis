## Key Lessons
- High-VIX regimes let low-volume sellers earn outsized returns as VIX collapses - the profit engine is vega plus view, no
- Spread risk across many strikes (6,500 through 8,500 puts) and multiple expiries (weekly/May/June) - never concentrate o
- Position-level losses (up to ~1.75 lakh single positions) coexist with net profit - judge the portfolio, not each leg.
- Never copy aggressive sizing: capital and risk appetite differ; matching the trade without matching the capital is ruino
- Staying flat or small is respectable - 'you must be happy as long as you are not losing money.'
- Markets can absorb good or bad news but not uncertainty; once an outcome is known, clarity itself re-prices risk.
- When 90% expect a fall, the fall is already priced - consensus trades usually fail.
- Option buyers can be right on direction and still lose when uncertainty resolves and premiums collapse.
- Look at everything - news, flows, regulation - never one indicator alone
- When a known buying window opens, front-runners buy in cash and short futures; late chart-followers get trapped
- Order-entry errors can crash markets 15% in minutes and reverse; stop-losses get hit both ways
- If one system reliably worked, everyone would follow it and it would stop working
- High collapsing VIX plus a clear directional view is where option sellers make windfall profits - not repeatable monthly
- Know when to exit: he closed nearly all positions after the Fibonacci target hit
- Markets factor in known bad news (efficient market) - shorting after visible bad news is how traders get squeezed
