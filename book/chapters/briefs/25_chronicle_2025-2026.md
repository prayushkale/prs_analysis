## Key lessons
- [2808] Differential tariffs destroy competitiveness even if uniform tariffs merely tax consumers: at 50% vs China's 10-20%, the Indian product is 25% dearer at the port - shipments get rejected and (in one export case) dumped at sea.
- [2808] A US appeals court ruled most Trump tariffs illegal with a stay to Oct 14 for Supreme Court appeal - headline relief, but economists note alternate legal routes keep the effective tariff alive.
- [3137] When futures premium decouples (225 points) from a crashing spot, either index-arb players are trapped or something structural (rebalance flows) is happening - do not average blindly
- [0845] Every ~2 years a crisis hits markets (Lehman 2008, Greece 2011, taper 2013, China 2015, demonetization 2016, Brexit 2017, COVID 2020) yet long-term India story stayed intact.
- [2808] When the whole world rallies and only your market falls, the cause is local: tariff isolation, rupee depreciation, FII outflows and a rising tax wedge - not global risk-off.
- [2755] An index 20% off its high is in a bear market regardless of the headline index - diversification across sectors is not diversification if one sector is 27% of the index.
- [2541] Monthly expiry plus pre-event exits plus the FII futures-to-cash reversal trade can stack into one violent session - carry only what a 300-point air pocket can survive.
- [3232] Even multi-bagger leaders (Reliance, Asian Paints, HDFC Bank) can deliver ~zero returns for 5-10 years after huge runs — time corrections follow price corrections.
- [2511] Rupee depreciation panics FIIs into selling; selling begets gap-downs begets more depreciation - a vicious cycle only policy or a giant domestic buyer can arrest.
- [2982] Governments ignore small market declines but respond violently to big ones (2019 corporate-tax cut, 2025 GST cut) - budget anticipation follows the same curve.
