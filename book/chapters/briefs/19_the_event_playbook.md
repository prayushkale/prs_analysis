## Key lessons
- [2808] Differential tariffs destroy competitiveness even if uniform tariffs merely tax consumers: at 50% vs China's 10-20%, the Indian product is 25% dearer at the port - shipments get rejected and (in one export case) dumped at sea.
- [0244] Even across seven major crises (Lehman, Greece, taper tantrum, China devaluation, demonetization+Trump, Brexit, Feb-2018 US flash crash), Nifty never gapped more than 400 points except Lehman and COVID; Bank Nifty max 1,200.
- [2808] A US appeals court ruled most Trump tariffs illegal with a stay to Oct 14 for Supreme Court appeal - headline relief, but economists note alternate legal routes keep the effective tariff alive.
- [0541] Markets rarely repeat the same fall for the same reason: second COVID wave took 1,200 points where the first took 40% - so 2013's 20% taper crash should not be the default expectation.
- [2253] Betting-market polls must be read cautiously: money-weighted (one whale can skew), tiny sample vs actual electors, and thresholds may not match the majority mark (300 vs 272).
- [3001] ITM put calendar: sell near-month put (collect premium), buy far-month put (protection) - best for budget when you expect near-term consolidation but want long-term protection
- [0845] Every ~2 years a crisis hits markets (Lehman 2008, Greece 2011, taper 2013, China 2015, demonetization 2016, Brexit 2017, COVID 2020) yet long-term India story stayed intact.
- [2808] When the whole world rallies and only your market falls, the cause is local: tariff isolation, rupee depreciation, FII outflows and a rising tax wedge - not global risk-off.
- [2237] At all-time highs, the question is not exit vs stay but whether the downside tail is hedged; stable-outcome probability was high but downside magnitude larger than upside.
- [2660] In crisis weeks the goal is capital preservation, not profit: sell far out-of-the-money options where premium compensates (e.g., ~6% OTM puts paying ~5-6% for 3 days).
