## Key Lessons
- Gold spikes when a global crisis forces central banks to slash rates - near-zero deposit rates make yieldless gold attra
- Whether to buy gold depends on your view of how long the crisis and the low-rate regime will last.
- ETFs combine fund diversification with stock-like tradability and tiny expense ratios
- For pledge-based traders, Nifty BeES collateral carries only ~5% haircut vs 10-20% for mutual funds
- Sovereign gold bonds are eligible collateral with about 10% haircut
- Falling interest-rate environments lift long-duration bond ETF prices - match duration to the rate view.
