## Key Lessons (by frequency)
- [1×] Reported finding: expiry-day SIPs historically beat fixed-date SIPs because expiry days skew negative.
- [1×] India lacks stock LEAPS (only Nifty offers 1-year options; Bank Nifty long-dated is illiquid) - warrants are the occasional workaround.
- [1×] Definitions for the print book: 0-10% swing = normal, 10-20% = correction, >20% = bear market.
- [1×] Dividend stripping (buy for dividend, book the price-drop loss, offset it) is expressly disallowed by tax rules.
- [1×] Dividends are taxed at your slab; short-term capital gains at a lower fixed rate - selling before the record date is often cheaper.
- [1×] If a deal looks like free money, it is already priced in - ask who is selling to you.
- [1×] Special dividends usually signal asset sales/wind-downs; the ex-dividend price drop can be near-total.
- [1×] Markets price the lead indicator (next few months) - current bad news is already in the price (efficient market: you are last to know).
