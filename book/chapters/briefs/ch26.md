## Key Lessons
- Trust only calls published before the level triggers; 'I said this morning in my private group' posts are retrofit scams
- India runs European-style options; early-exercise assignment would make hedged books unmanageable overnight.
- Always square off before expiry: stock options and futures devolve into physical delivery.
- Stock lending & borrowing survives mainly as a delivery-failure backstop because stock futures replaced shorting-in-cash
- Edge cannot be taught - it is the experience of reading the same news differently; hedge can.
- Option selling is a book of many trades: a few failures drown in the aggregate profit - the insurance-pool principle.
