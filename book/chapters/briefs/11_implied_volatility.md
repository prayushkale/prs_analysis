## Key lessons
- [0244] Even across seven major crises (Lehman, Greece, taper tantrum, China devaluation, demonetization+Trump, Brexit, Feb-2018 US flash crash), Nifty never gapped more than 400 points except Lehman and COVID; Bank Nifty max 1,200.
- [0135] Vaccine/normalization news deflates the fear premium: metals crashed the day after Russia's August 2020 vaccine announcement while old-economy stocks with 'bad results' rallied.
- [0030] In violent moves, premiums behave abnormally because losing-side sellers are forced to square up and roll - not because the market 'should' behave that way.
- [0219] Weekly expiries existed on Nifty and Bank Nifty while stock options had only the monthly expiry; long-dated (5-year) index options exist but are illiquid.
- [0135] Falling IV collapses both call and put premiums - long-dated option buyers harvest theta-and-IV decay; position for IV regime, not direction alone.
- [0135] Gold's all-time highs get made during systemic crises and can take decades to revisit (1980 peak held until 2008; post-2011 high ran to $1070).
- [1922] The ATM straddle premium as % of spot IS the market's expected move: sellers profit if the actual move stays inside it, buyers only outside it.
- [0244] Implied volatility is almost always higher than historical volatility - paying full straddle premium around events is structurally losing.
- [0024] Call buyers profit when market rises, put buyers when it falls - but volatility and time decay can still turn a right view into a loss.
- [1470] When IV spikes, exchanges raise margins: option sellers' returns collapse to ~0.5% while buyers face huge breakevens - both sides lose.
