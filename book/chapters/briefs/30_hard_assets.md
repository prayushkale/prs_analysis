## Key lessons
- [0111] Time decay is proportional to remaining time: a one-week option loses ~20% of its value on day one, but ~100% on expiry day - expiry-day selling harvests decay that weekly sellers spread across days.
- [0111] India charges full span+exposure margin on covered calls against demat shares (no cash-F&O link); holding a long future instead cuts the short call's span to zero - only exposure margin applies.
- [0111] Bond yields falling means older bonds trade at a premium (Bharat Bond 1 near 1080-1090 vs 1000 face); pledging older listed bonds can beat waiting for new issues to clear collateral approval.
- [0210] A durable, dominant media franchise can trade at a fraction of peer multiples when saddled with a legal/political overhang - the discount itself is the opportunity if the overhang clears.
- [0106] There is no safe strategy and no permanently fundamentally strong company - Reliance Communications/Infrastructure/Capital were once blue chips; understand risk and reward per strategy.
- [0135] Vaccine/normalization news deflates the fear premium: metals crashed the day after Russia's August 2020 vaccine announcement while old-economy stocks with 'bad results' rallied.
- [0140] Dividend-yield investing is a patience game: price declines raise the yield and attract institutions - the thesis strengthens as the price falls (if the dividend sustains).
- [2698] The government's biggest gold lesson is its own: selling Sovereign Gold Bonds unhedged at 2,500-2,700/gram against a price near 10,000 is a naked short position gone wrong.
- [2698] Regulation that ignores ground reality shifts activity to unregulated lenders: capping organized gold loans at 75% LTV pushes desperate borrowers to 20-30% moneylenders.
- [2698] India's gold price is international price + rupee depreciation + import duty/GST; high duty spread (18%) is what funds smuggling, so duty cuts shrink the black channel.
