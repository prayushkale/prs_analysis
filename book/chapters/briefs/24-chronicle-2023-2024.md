## Core lessons
1. After a normal correction within an uptrend, the odds favor resumption; watch when persistent FII sellers flip - that flip drives the next leg.
2. An index is not the market: with ~54% of Nifty weight in seven stocks, a handful of drags can mask broad strength - and their recovery alone adds hundreds of points.
3. Compare flow size to context: four months of FII selling equalled one strong buying month, so the market held.
4. Rising retail participation (IPO subscriptions, exchange/broker stock prices) can absorb institutional selling.
5. Long-run equity return tracks nominal growth, not headline economy quality - Japan vs India counter-example.
6. After a 100% post-crash rally, the historical pattern is continuation, not collapse - 2005-07 tripled with multiple 10% corrections en route.
7. Low rates ignite the capex cycle: leverage expands EPS, bull markets expand the multiple - price = EPS x PE can double from both sides.
8. Profits rotate: index-doublers book into physical assets, real estate revives, and the wealth effect returns to equities.
