## Key Lessons
- Implied volatility is almost always higher than historical volatility - paying full straddle premium around events is st
- Across 10 budgets, Nifty never closed more than 2% from the prior close except the COVID-coinciding 2020 budget (-3.26%)
- Even across seven major crises (Lehman, Greece, taper tantrum, China devaluation, demonetization+Trump, Brexit, Feb-2018
- Back spreads cut max loss by a third versus long straddles and win big only on large moves in one direction.
- If worried about uncapped ratio-spread risk, buy one far OTM option and convert to a butterfly.
- In a butterfly, buys and sells must balance (1-5-4) — only then is risk limited and margin benefits apply.
