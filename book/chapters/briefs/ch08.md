## Strategies
- Futures-plus-collateral long-term long: Buy 1 lot long futures; park notional value in FD/Bharat Bond; pledge for collateral margin  (Reliance example: ~7.5L notional; premium ~Rs 5/share/month (~30k/yr); interest )
- Synthetic futures (long/short): Long: buy ATM call + sell ATM put (same expiry). Short: reverse legs  (Demo: June futures -222 vs synthetic -219 on a 222-point fall; cost base 7.5 lak)
- Covered interest carry trade (1995 example): Borrow SGD at 3%, place INR NRNR deposit at 20%, hedge FX with a 3-year forward costing ~2%/yr  (gross spread 17%; net ~15% riskless; 10 lakh -> ~17.5 lakh over 3 years)
