## Key Lessons
- The first weeks of January set the year's institutional-flow trend - watch FII allocation behaviour then
- In high-volatility regimes, selling far-OTM weekly wings (~₹10 premiums) earns roughly 1% per week on margin
- Folklore: a green first session of the year augurs a good year (he flags it as sentiment, not data)
- January's first two weeks of FII flows are a leading indicator for the full year (2011 precedent)
- Gap-downs usually recover, but FIIs sell the recovery - expect a second leg before support holds
- High-dividend PSUs plus covered calls can target 20-25% p.a. without betting on price appreciation
- US market history: years that start with a lower close tend to follow with ~15% gains (cited as past data, not promise)
- As a trader, never average a stock that has already fallen 50% - after 50% the odds of another deep leg rise
- SGB + annual 2.5% interest + covered calls on MCX gold ≈ 20-25% annualized on gold exposure
- FIIs pre-build options (buy puts, sell calls) then trigger bulk selling on Wednesdays/Thursdays around expiry
- When four heavyweights cause half the index fall, read it as targeted institutional exit, not broad selling
- Falling crude is structurally positive for India (inflation, OMCs, autos) even when caused by global weakness
- In a rangebound market the trend follower waits; the seller sells wings - each gets paid for patience
- When a well-watched support breaks, the fall is vertical - hedges must exist before, not after
- Green mid/small caps against a falling index = concentrated institutional exit from large caps, not panic
