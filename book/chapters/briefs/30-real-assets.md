## Core lessons
1. Gold spikes when a global crisis forces central banks to slash rates - near-zero deposit rates make yieldless gold attractive.
2. Whether to buy gold depends on your view of how long the crisis and the low-rate regime will last.
3. ETFs combine fund diversification with stock-like tradability and tiny expense ratios
4. For pledge-based traders, Nifty BeES collateral carries only ~5% haircut vs 10-20% for mutual funds
5. Sovereign gold bonds are eligible collateral with about 10% haircut
6. Falling interest-rate environments lift long-duration bond ETF prices - match duration to the rate view.
7. A 0.0005% expense ratio compounds into materially higher post-tax yield versus active debt funds.
8. Listed bond ETFs double as high-quality collateral (5% haircut), making idle capital productive for trading.

## Strategies
- **Bharat Bond as trading collateral**: Buy Bharat Bond ETF units; pledge to broker as collateral for F&O margins
- **Liquid BeES cash-component collateral**: Buy Liquid BeES; pledge as the cash component of margin
- **Collateral-funded option selling (HNI pattern)**: Pledge debt mutual funds/bank FDs as collateral; sell options against the margin; keep interest as primary return
- **Reverse arbitrage on futures discount**: Sell physical shares held in demat, buy the discounted futures; converge at expiry and reverse both
- **Covered call via futures leg (margin-optimized)**: Hold long futures instead of physical shares; sell calls against it
