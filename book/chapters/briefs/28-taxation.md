## Core lessons
1. Donation exemptions cannot shelter trading income: 100% exemption applies only within the 10%-of-profit cap (except the PM Cares ordinance window).
2. Donations to approved PM/CM funds: 100% deduction; most NGOs: 50% deduction - both subject to the qualifying-limit rule.
3. For large capital, long futures funded by pledged debt instruments beats cash buying: interest income exceeds the cost-of-carry premium.
4. A short call against a long futures position needs only incremental margin - cash-market holders pay far more to run the same hedge.
5. Leverage is not inherently gambling: buying with Rs 100 while committing only Rs 20, keeping the rest safe, is capital efficiency.
6. Futures gains are business income (expenses deductible); cash equity gets 10% LTCG / 15% STCG - pick the instrument with the tax profile you prefer.
7. Keep roughly your-age-percent in debt; deploy lump sums via SIP, withdraw via SWP.
8. Trade indices, not stocks: ITM stock options that lapse trigger physical delivery; index options are cash-settled.

## Strategies
- **Futures-plus-collateral long-term long**: Buy 1 lot long futures; park notional value in FD/Bharat Bond; pledge for collateral margin
- **Covered call via futures leg (margin-optimized)**: Hold long futures instead of physical shares; sell calls against it
- **Collateral ladder for option sellers**: Pledge Liquid BeES/debt funds (~90% collateral value); keep a small cash buffer for debit legs
