## Key Lessons
- Contracts expiring into physical delivery can print absurd prices when storage is exhausted - know delivery mechanics be

- Look at everything - news, flows, regulation - never one indicator alone

- Order-entry errors can crash markets 15% in minutes and reverse; stop-losses get hit both ways
