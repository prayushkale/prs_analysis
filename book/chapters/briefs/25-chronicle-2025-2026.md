## Core lessons
1. Fracking made the US the world's top producer and permanently weakened OPEC's production-cut pricing power.
2. With 5% margin, a 1% adverse move costs 20% of capital - commodity leverage, not commodity direction, is what destroys traders.
3. In a crash, a 100-rupee stock can go to 5; a 100-dollar commodity cannot go to 1 - commodities have a floor equities lack.
4. Prices of telecom and airfares fell/stayed flat for 30 years while everything else inflated - technology and competition deflate industries.
5. Regional real estate tracks the economics of its buyers: Kerala property softened as Gulf oil economies stalled.
6. Crash depth sets rebound height: 100%+ index years occurred only after 40-50% crashes, so deep-fall years are the rebound candidates.
7. Long-dated wide strangles monetize the VIX-implied annual range without intraday stress.
8. Domestic SIP flows (~1.2 lakh crore/year) can mathematically absorb another year of record FII selling.
