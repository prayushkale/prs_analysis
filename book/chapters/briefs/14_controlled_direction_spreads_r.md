## Key Lessons (by frequency)
- [1×] In violent markets, far-OTM options can move opposite to the index because traders buy them to cut margin - premium behavior beats price intuition.
- [1×] After a global selloff, any good news triggers violent short covering - position for the squeeze, not the narrative.
- [1×] Small-net-credit ratio structures can carry 81% probability with a few-thousand-rupee payoff - size for the risk, not the reward.
- [1×] When everything hinges on one event, that event is the only variable worth tracking (budget, budget, budget).
- [1×] Option buying needs real predictive skill - Thales predicted the harvest before buying the presses; random call/put buying loses uniformly.
- [1×] In 25 years Nifty rarely fell 4 consecutive months; after 3-4 red months the next month rallied, a repeatable seasonal stat.
- [1×] Financing a long call by selling a farther put cuts cost and wins both ways if right - but loses both ways if wrong.
- [1×] The ATM straddle premium as % of spot IS the market's expected move: sellers profit if the actual move stays inside it, buyers only outside it.
