## Core lessons
1. An option is asymmetric betting: buyer risks only the premium; seller pays 1:1 per point beyond the strike.
2. Premium moves on expectation, not just price - a batter racing toward a century raises the 'premium' before the century exists.
3. Call buyers profit when market rises, put buyers when it falls - but volatility and time decay can still turn a right view into a loss.
4. Buyers can exit before expiry; profit-taking need not wait for settlement.
5. In a falling-VIX environment, correct directional views still lose money - vega swamps delta for option buyers.
6. Weekly, monthly and far-month puts ALL fell or stagnated during a 500-point drop - the effect is structural, not decay noise.
7. Do not buy naked options when VIX is falling; run structures that own a short premium leg.
8. Television-expert option-buying advice during high VIX is exactly the trap to avoid.
