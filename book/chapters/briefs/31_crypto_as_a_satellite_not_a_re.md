## Key Lessons (by frequency)
- [1×] Gold spikes only in genuine currency crises (1980 oil shock, 2008 Lehman); 2020 broke the pattern because stablecoins now do that hedging job.
- [1×] Indian gold price has three drivers: international price, USD/INR rate, and local taxation (high tax breeds smuggling).
- [1×] INR was stable 70-75 from 2013-2021 and was Asia's strongest currency 2014-2021; the 72-to-80 slide need not persist.
- [1×] If no major fall happened, the market cannot rebound 100% - the rebound matches the depth of the fall.
- [1×] Crypto F&O is treated as business income, allowing netting across coins and expense offset - changing the practical economics.
- [1×] New crypto venues suffer thin liquidity and wide bid-ask impact cost until participation grows.
- [1×] CFD brokers earn via widened spreads (~1 rupee each side in his example), not brokerage; zero brokerage is not free money.
