## Key Lessons
- Markets bottom after big panic selling (capitulation); overreaction to one name often marks a multi-week low.
- Panic in one bond/stock drags the whole sector down with it - contagion is mechanical, not fundamental.
- Traders who understand irrationality can profit during it; accumulation often starts at maximum pessimism.
- High collapsing VIX plus a clear directional view is where option sellers make windfall profits - not repeatable monthly
- Know when to exit: he closed nearly all positions after the Fibonacci target hit
- Markets factor in known bad news (efficient market) - shorting after visible bad news is how traders get squeezed
