- Crypto trading is not speculative income — you receive actual tokens, unlike F&O — so business-expense deductions apply.
- Mining is a business: electricity and incidentals are deductible, computers depreciated as capital assets.
- Taxable income above ₹50 lakh requires ITR-2 asset disclosures, including crypto holdings bought with rupees (not 'forei
- Crypto premiums are ultra-high: the same far-OTM strikes that pay pennies on index options paid thousands of dollars.
- Exchange P&L reports counted premium credits as profit; true P&L must be computed from wallet balance minus deposits.
