## Key Lessons
- Contracts expiring into physical delivery can print absurd prices when storage is exhausted - know delivery mechanics be
- Negative prices were contract-specific: the next-month contract kept trading at normal positive levels.
- Look at everything - news, flows, regulation - never one indicator alone
- When a known buying window opens, front-runners buy in cash and short futures; late chart-followers get trapped
- Order-entry errors can crash markets 15% in minutes and reverse; stop-losses get hit both ways
- If one system reliably worked, everyone would follow it and it would stop working
- Return on margin matters, not how much the underlying moves - index gives 6-7% on margin vs 4% for a stock moving twice 
- Stocks can gap 50-70% overnight; indices are circuit-protected
- Index options keep liquidity in far strikes and later months; stock options do not
- No physical delivery headache in index options
- Resting limit orders far from spot can get filled by flash spikes; square off immediately - such dislocations vanish in
