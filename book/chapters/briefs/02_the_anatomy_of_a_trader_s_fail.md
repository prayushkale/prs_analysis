## Strategy Setups
- **FD-interest ATM call roll (annual)**: 1 crore in FD (~7 lakh interest); each year after a 10%+ correction, spend the interest on December-expiry ATM calls
  - Risk: zero in flat years; multiple consecutive zeros possible
  - Best regime: capital-heavy investors with long horizons and corrections present
- **Partial-profit + free-carry discipline**: Book ~50% of gains once position doubles; remainder rides free
  - Risk: opportunity cost on the sold half
  - Best regime: any trending position
- **Zero-cost portfolio collar**: Buy Nifty ETF (entry ~24,000) + buy Dec-2025 24,000 put + sell 28,000 call with equal premium
  - Risk: caps upside above the sold call strike; basis risk between ETF and index
  - Best regime: euphoric markets when you want to stay invested but cap downside
- **Pair trade (long stock / short index future)**: Hold a strong stock long; short index futures of equal value using pledged collateral
  - Risk: stock-specific underperformance vs index
  - Best regime: neutral-to-bearish markets while retaining stock conviction
- **Trend-following allocation mix**: Split capital ~4 ways: two non-directional (straddle-family) strategies + two directional (trend-following) strategies of differen
  - Risk: Directional books suffer in violent news-driven reversals; non-directional suffe
  - Best regime: All regimes - the mix, not the signal, is the risk control
