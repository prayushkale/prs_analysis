## Key Lessons (by frequency)
- [1×] India's F&O-to-cash turnover ratio is the world's highest - the regulatory trigger for margin tightening.
- [1×] Broker intraday leverage was phased out: extra limit cut from multiples to one-third by June 2021, then to zero.
- [1×] Removing intraday sellers shrinks supply, so option premiums rise - sellers were compensated, not punished.
- [1×] Peak-margin penalties demand funds the instant a loss prints - the rule traders could legitimately contest.
- [1×] Money does not vanish when a rule changes: broker pools migrate to funding proven traders instead of retail leverage.
- [1×] If you truly profit intraday, brokers will fund your capital - claiming otherwise proves the skill is not there.
- [1×] India lacks stock LEAPS (only Nifty offers 1-year options; Bank Nifty long-dated is illiquid) - warrants are the occasional workaround.
- [1×] Definitions for the print book: 0-10% swing = normal, 10-20% = correction, >20% = bear market.
