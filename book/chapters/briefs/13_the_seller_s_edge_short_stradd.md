## Strategy Setups
- **Calendar inversion (long near-week straddle, short far-week straddle)**: Buy ATM call+put current weekly expiry; sell ATM call+put next weekly expiry
  - Risk: Absolute sideways market decays both longs; near-week theta bleed
  - Best regime: Expecting violent moves (event seasons)
- **Far-OTM safe option selling (fragmentary)**: Sell distant-month far OTM options with ~10% extra distance beyond the nominal safe strike
  - Risk: unrecoverable (tail loss if level breaks)
  - Best regime: High IV with strong support/resistance shelves
- **Infosys earnings iron fly with financed wings**: Sell 650CE + 650PE (5 lots); buy 720CE + 580PE; sell 2x 750CE (10 lots) and 2x 550PE to finance the wings
  - Risk: Beyond 750/550 the financing legs turn the risk unlimited again; overnight gap r
  - Best regime: Results eve with elevated implied volatility
- **Cash-secured puts (ICICI 300PE, HDFC 840PE)**: Sell puts below market with full cash set aside for delivery
  - Risk: sharp breakdown below strike forces delivery at loss
  - Best regime: moderately bullish with stocks near support
- **Put ratio spread (9100PE bought / 8500PE sold)**: Long lower put, short further-OTM puts
  - Risk: accelerated loss beyond short strikes
  - Best regime: mildly bearish to flat
