## Key Lessons
- VIX measures expected one-month movement; it can fall in a falling market when the fall is narrow (heavyweights only, br
- Monthly expected move = VIX / sqrt(12); place short strikes beyond that band (example: VIX 60 -> ~17% band).
- VIX is derived from option prices - the straddle premium is a live cross-check of the VIX-implied range.
- High VIX favors sellers who keep distance; low VIX means the market is priced for calm and strikes must come closer.
- Compute FII long/short percentages from NSE participant-wise open-interest reports instead of relying on TV sound bites.
- Extreme FII index-short positioning (~85%) historically marks bottoms; read index and stock futures together for the tru
- Futures: daily MTM cash transfer. Options: buyer pays premium once; seller's margin floats with the option's price.
- Expiry settlement price is the weighted average of the last half hour - an ITM option can expire worthless despite a fav
- Hedged option positions carry roughly one-third the margin of naked shorts (June 2020 regime).
- Crowd consensus is dangerous: markets are structured so only the minority profits.
- Confirmation bias: when you want a crash you will find reasons - trade the market that is, not the pattern that 'should'
- Options-seller MTM losses can be adjusted against collateral until booked; futures MTM must be paid in cash daily - know
- Squaring only the hedge leg instantly recreates a naked position: margin shortfall penalties run 0.5%/day (<1 lakh), 1%/
- There is no holy-grail directional formula in open interest - only guidelines plus experience.
- With 40-50k capital, F&O is a losing game by structure - buy mutual funds instead.
