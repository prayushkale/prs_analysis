## Key Lessons
- High-VIX regimes let low-volume sellers earn outsized returns as VIX collapses - the profit engine is vega plus view, no
- Spread risk across many strikes (6,500 through 8,500 puts) and multiple expiries (weekly/May/June) - never concentrate o
- Position-level losses (up to ~1.75 lakh single positions) coexist with net profit - judge the portfolio, not each leg.
- Never copy aggressive sizing: capital and risk appetite differ; matching the trade without matching the capital is ruino
- Staying flat or small is respectable - 'you must be happy as long as you are not losing money.'
- Markets can absorb good or bad news but not uncertainty; once an outcome is known, clarity itself re-prices risk.

## Strategies
- Strike-and-expiry put ladder (illustrative, high risk): Sold puts at 6,500 / 7,000 / 7,300 / 7,400 / 7,500 / 7,800 / 8,500 across weekly, May and June expir  (Capital ~95 lakh -> ~45 lakh profit in 6 sessions (~50%); sold Jun 7,000PE @475,)
- Multi-strike, multi-expiry put selling (April 2020): Sold put options spread across various strike prices and expiry periods rather than one strike  (7500PE sold around Rs.75 during a morning dip; SGX Nifty ~9200 vs ~9000 close)
- Put-selling ladder in collapsing-VIX rebound: Sold puts across strikes from ~6,100 to 9,000 during April 2020 recovery  (April profit 66,30,478 on ~95L capital; VIX 80-85 -> 55-60 -> ~40; June 9000PE s)
