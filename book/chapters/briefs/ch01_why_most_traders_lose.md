## Key Lessons (from analysis)
- Markets bottom after big panic selling (capitulation); overreaction to one name often marks a multi-week low.
- Panic in one bond/stock drags the whole sector down with it - contagion is mechanical, not fundamental.
- Traders who understand irrationality can profit during it; accumulation often starts at maximum pessimism.
- For every option bought one is sold - if buying were strictly better, no seller would exist.
- Seller pays margin as collateral (FDs/shares), so cash outlay can be near zero; buyer pays premium in cash.
- Probability decides long-run winners: buyer wins in only one of five market scenarios, seller in most.
