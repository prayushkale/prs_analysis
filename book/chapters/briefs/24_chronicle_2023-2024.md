## Key lessons
- [1510] Open interest is guidance, not golden rules: sellers who collected big premium early stay profitable even beyond their strike - and gamma can make sellers 'terribly wrong'.
- [2351a] Binary-event decision table: 50bp + hawkish tone, 50bp + dovish, 25bp + hawkish, 25bp + dovish each imply a different gap - size for the worst cell, not the base case.
- [1517] Seasonality with a cause: capitulation months cluster, and the forced-deleveraging end tends to produce a relief candle - but demand confirmation before trusting it.
- [0518] An index is not the market: with ~54% of Nifty weight in seven stocks, a handful of drags can mask broad strength - and their recovery alone adds hundreds of points.
- [1813] Ratings actions on US regional banks are sentiment shocks for Indian banks, yet Indian financials can decouple the same day - trade the local tape, not the headline.
- [1645] Ahead of binary events, professionals carry minimal positions - the reaction to the central banker's press conference often matters more than the rate decision.
- [2346a] When India VIX rises inside a rally: call premiums inflate while put premiums stay bid - traders are paying up for upside protection and downside put-selling.
- [2132] Intraday traders must trade only the deepest liquidity (Nifty, then Bank Nifty) - stop-loss jumps in thin index options can erase 10-20% of capital in minutes
- [1660] Institutional tug-of-war (FII buy vs DII sell) generates a repeating intraday cycle: gap, sell-off, FII entry, short-covering - trade the cycle, not the news.
- [1493] Build asymmetric payoff structures: lose only above the all-time high while collecting along the way - structure the trade so the market must prove you wrong.
