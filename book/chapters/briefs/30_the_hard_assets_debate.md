## Strategy Setups
- **Collateral-funded option selling (HNI pattern)**: Pledge debt mutual funds/bank FDs as collateral; sell options against the margin; keep interest as primary return
  - Risk: Trading losses eat the collateral cushion; interest is not guaranteed against cr
  - Best regime: Wealthy, income-focused sellers who accept trading as a bonus stream
- **Reverse arbitrage on futures discount**: Sell physical shares held in demat, buy the discounted futures; converge at expiry and reverse both
  - Risk: Only works if shares already held; capital locked; convergence timing
  - Best regime: Futures at significant discount (e.g., Nifty futures 80-90 points under spot)
- **Bharat Bond collateral pledge**: Buy Bharat Bond ETF tranche (e.g., April 2033, ~7.5% YTM), pledge to the exchange for F&O margin, trade against it
  - Risk: Interest-rate mark-to-market before maturity; 1-2 month pledge-eligibility lag f
  - Best regime: High-rate environments locking long tenure; traders wanting low-risk collateral 
- **Bullion index equity hedge**: Long/short equity portfolio paired with same-direction or inverse position in MCX BULLDEX futures (70.52% gold / 29.48% silver)
  - Risk: Correlation breakdown; VaR-driven margin changes
  - Best regime: Portfolio-level diversification when equity-gold inverse correlation is intact
- **Sector momentum with hedged pledging framework**: SIP into PSU Bank ETF; pledge units for F&O limit; hedge portfolio with Bank Nifty options (long-dated where liquid)
  - Risk: Sector reversal; long-dated Bank Nifty options illiquid beyond 2-3 months
  - Best regime: Early credit-cycle recovery after NPA recognition cycle; rate-neutral economy
