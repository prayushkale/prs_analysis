## Key Lessons (from analysis)
- High-VIX regimes let low-volume sellers earn outsized returns as VIX collapses - the profit engine is vega plus view, not volume.
- Spread risk across many strikes (6,500 through 8,500 puts) and multiple expiries (weekly/May/June) - never concentrate one strike.
- Position-level losses (up to ~1.75 lakh single positions) coexist with net profit - judge the portfolio, not each leg.
- Never copy aggressive sizing: capital and risk appetite differ; matching the trade without matching the capital is ruinous.
- Staying flat or small is respectable - 'you must be happy as long as you are not losing money.'
- Markets can absorb good or bad news but not uncertainty; once an outcome is known, clarity itself re-prices risk.
