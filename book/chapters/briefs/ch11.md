## Key Lessons
- An option is asymmetric betting: buyer risks only the premium; seller pays 1:1 per point beyond the strike.
- Premium moves on expectation, not just price - a batter racing toward a century raises the 'premium' before the century 
- Call buyers profit when market rises, put buyers when it falls - but volatility and time decay can still turn a right vi
- Buyers can exit before expiry; profit-taking need not wait for settlement.
- In a falling-VIX environment, correct directional views still lose money - vega swamps delta for option buyers.
- Weekly, monthly and far-month puts ALL fell or stagnated during a 500-point drop - the effect is structural, not decay n
