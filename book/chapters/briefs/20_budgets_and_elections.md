## Key lessons
- [2793] GST registration thresholds (40 lakh goods, 20 lakh services) force tiny businesses - a 40-lakh-turnover vendor nets ~20,000/month presumptive profit - to act as unpaid collectors; Singapore's threshold is many multiples higher.
- [2796] Tax history cuts one way: LTCG went 0 -> 10 -> 12.5%, STCG 10 -> 15 -> 20%, options STT up ~5x, dividend moved from 20% DDT to up to 42% in-hand for HNIs, indexation removed - the 'transaction cost' narrative FIIs cite is real.
- [2793] Rationalizing 5/12/18/28 slabs to essentially 5/18 moves most 28% goods (ACs, refrigerators, washing machines) to 18% - a direct ~10-point consumption stimulus that lifted auto stocks ~4-5% to all-time highs the next session.
- [2796] The 2019 playbook: FPI surcharge outraged FIIs, the government U-turned and cut corporate tax 30% to 22% in one stroke; Nifty jumped ~10% in two sessions and trapped short sellers - policy panic can be the bull case.
- [2796] An RBI deputy governor's white paper once called 2-lakh-crore FII selling a potential black swan; the current episode is twice that size, with retail SIPs (~28,000 crore/month) as the absorption engine.
- [2796] Every pressure on the government historically produces market-friendly shocks; the wishlist is STT reduction, LTCG removal, and fuel-price cuts - all broader and simpler than targeted sops.
- [2796] Losses can exceed capital: a 50-lakh-capital trader selling calls into that rally lost 75 lakh by day's end and had to pump in more - never assume max loss equals your account balance.
- [2793] Sin goods are structured as GST plus compensation cess (cigarettes/carbonated drinks 28+17=45%; SUVs 28+22=50%); reform takes them to ~40%, keeping the vice-penalty logic intact.
- [2793] Broad-based relief (fuel price cuts) beats narrow ones (income-tax slab hikes from 7 to 12 lakh help only a thin band) - every consumer and vegetable vendor rides the same bus.
- [2253] Betting-market polls must be read cautiously: money-weighted (one whale can skew), tiny sample vs actual electors, and thresholds may not match the majority mark (300 vs 272).
