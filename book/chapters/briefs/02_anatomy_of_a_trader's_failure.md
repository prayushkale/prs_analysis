## Key lessons
- [0304] A stock makes 90% of its move in 10% of the time - boredom is the price of holding (Reliance flat 2008-2017, then +400% in 3 years; a power stock flat a decade, then 3x in 3 months).
- [0304] Trainers monetize the desperate: overpriced software (Rs60,000/yr, Rs30,000 commission) and fictitious model portfolios (Rs3L to Rs40L, '2000%') target people who just lost 90%.
- [0132] Removing the protective long leg on expiry day converts your short into a naked position - margin and risk jump; close positions yourself rather than letting settlement decide.
- [0132] Capital gates: avoid stock options without ~1 crore (adjustment margins), avoid option selling below ~10 lakh; stick to large-cap liquidity to stay clear of ban-list freezes.
- [0117] Derivatives are a negative-sum game: both winner and loser pay brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange charges and taxes (~Rs 50,000 cr/yr drained; STT alone ~Rs 6,000 cr).
- [1944] Benchmark the decision: full-time trading must beat (trading return minus delegation cost) plus your salary - else keep the job and trade long-term options part-time.
- [1318] Selective posting of one account while an opposite account absorbs losses is the classic fake-P&L structure - audit capital growth and lifestyle, not screenshots.
- [1074] Leverage needs (long via margin funding, short via SLB) already exist without stock futures - and SLB would pay lenders (mutual funds, hence retail) extra yield.
- [1316] A manager trading his own account with near-zero negotiated costs can show 70%+ annual returns that are structurally impossible for clients paying retail costs.
- [1322] Mean reversion breaks on corporate actions: REC's fall from 140 to 105 was a 1:3 bonus adjustment, not a correction - the retracement trade expired worthless.
