## Key Lessons
- Rolling long futures costs ~5-6% a year in premium; long-term positions belong in cash, shorts earn the premium.
- Stock lending & borrowing loses to futures shorting: no futures premium, borrowing cost, and full option margin on the h
- Market-wide position limit above 95% bans a stock; the ban persists until it falls below 80% - even on expiry day.
- Margin money is calibrated to cover ~99% of moves: 2 short Nifty lots lose only ~35-40K in a 500-point gap against 1L+ m
- Corrections are -10 to -20%, bear territory beyond -20% - label drawdowns by definition, not by fear.
- India VIX at multi-year lows is the most dangerous regime for pure option sellers - one VIX spike erases months of theta
- In low VIX, always pair buys with sells so a volatility spike cushions both legs.
- Sell the in-the-money strike and hedge in a far month, not the adjacent month, when running put calendars.
- A calendar's max loss is bounded by the debit structure even in a crash - that is the point of the hedge.
- Markets don't crash twice for the same reason: first time is a surprise, second is experience (2013 taper −20% vs 2021 t
- Central banks now sugar-coat tightening: pair every negative with a positive, and taper talk precedes taper by months.
- A crash portfolio: ~60% index ETFs, ~10% gold (crisis hedge — gold rose in 2008 and 2020), ~30% debt/REITs you can liqui
- Combine a call ratio (buy 1, sell 2 far) with a cash-secured put: no downside loss in a wide band, uncapped-ish profit i
- Nifty's long-run average is ~9%/yr since 1995 — 50%+ years follow 40-50% crashes; do not extrapolate boom years.
- Crypto IV is extreme: next-month BTC straddles price ~20-25% of spot vs 5-6% Bank Nifty, 3-4% Nifty.
