## Key Lessons
- Rolling long futures costs ~5-6% a year in premium; long-term positions belong in cash, shorts earn the premium.
- Stock lending & borrowing loses to futures shorting: no futures premium, borrowing cost, and full option margin on the h
- Market-wide position limit above 95% bans a stock; the ban persists until it falls below 80% - even on expiry day.
- Margin money is calibrated to cover ~99% of moves: 2 short Nifty lots lose only ~35-40K in a 500-point gap against 1L+ m
- Corrections are -10 to -20%, bear territory beyond -20% - label drawdowns by definition, not by fear.
- India VIX at multi-year lows is the most dangerous regime for pure option sellers - one VIX spike erases months of theta
