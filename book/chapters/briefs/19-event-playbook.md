## Core lessons
1. Enter earnings trades the day before results and exit once the event resolves; hold further only with a stop-loss.
2. Finance protective wings by selling double quantity of farther strikes so the hedge costs nothing from pocket.
3. A naked short straddle is high risk; the same view becomes limited-risk when converted to an iron fly.
4. Hedged option combos earn margin benefits - selling both sides costs far less margin than the sum of separate legs.
5. Option selling is about safely handling large capital, not about flashy percentage returns.
6. Exit near legs once the event resolves; keep far wings only with a stop-loss.
7. For high-IV stocks, sell the next-month straddle - richer premium with relatively smaller risk - and hedge with current-month wings.
8. Results timing matters: after-hours declarations carry gap risk, intraday declarations let you react.
