## Key Lessons
- Compute FII long/short percentages from NSE participant-wise open-interest reports instead of relying on TV sound bites.
- Extreme FII index-short positioning (~85%) historically marks bottoms; read index and stock futures together for the tru
- Option sellers pledge debt funds/FDs/liquid funds as collateral instead of paying cash margin
- Exchange collateral haircuts can jump to 100% overnight - pledged positions then force-liquidate
- Whatever happens anywhere in the world, the stock market feels it first
- Success needs two things: an edge (view/skill) and a hedge.
