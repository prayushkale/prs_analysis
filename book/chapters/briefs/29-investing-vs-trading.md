## Core lessons
1. Collateral eligibility depends on custody form: demat shares/MFs pledge instantly; folio-form MFs must convert first (2-4 weeks).
2. Futures MTM losses are cash-settled daily; option-selling MTM losses can be adjusted against collateral until booked.
3. Booked losses beyond accumulated premium force cash top-up - collateral protects MTM, not realized losses.
4. Depositories (NSDL/CDSL) hold shares; AMCs hold folio MFs; form determines pledgeability.
5. Markets price the lead indicator (next few months) - current bad news is already in the price (efficient market: you are last to know).
6. Very good news invites profit booking; very bad news invites policy rescue - so both can be sell/buy signals respectively.
7. Consensus is dangerous: when every retail message is bearish, the short trade is already crowded.
8. Defensive sectors (FMCG/IT/pharma) can rally through market-worst periods - index-level gloom hides stock-level bull markets.

## Strategies
- **FD/collateral-backed premium recycling**: ~25 lakh FD pledged; premiums from option selling recycled to absorb small booked losses without touching the FD
- **Cash Secured Put**: Sell a put at the strike where you want to own the stock; keep the full cash ready for delivery
- **Staggered index investing with cash-secured puts**: SIP into a Nifty 50 ETF; optionally place cash-secured puts at desired lower entries
- **Futures-plus-collateral long-term long**: Buy 1 lot long futures; park notional value in FD/Bharat Bond; pledge for collateral margin
- **Bharat Bond as trading collateral**: Buy Bharat Bond ETF units; pledge to broker as collateral for F&O margins
