## Core lessons
1. Implied volatility is almost always higher than historical volatility - paying full straddle premium around events is structurally losing.
2. Across 10 budgets, Nifty never closed more than 2% from the prior close except the COVID-coinciding 2020 budget (-3.26%).
3. Even across seven major crises (Lehman, Greece, taper tantrum, China devaluation, demonetization+Trump, Brexit, Feb-2018 US flash crash), Nifty never gapped more than 400 points except Lehman and COVID; Bank Nifty max 1,200.
4. Back spreads cut max loss by a third versus long straddles and win big only on large moves in one direction.
5. If worried about uncapped ratio-spread risk, buy one far OTM option and convert to a butterfly.
6. In a butterfly, buys and sells must balance (1-5-4) — only then is risk limited and margin benefits apply.
7. Anchor strategy legs to the stock's all-time high: profit zone below the ATH, limited loss above it.
8. A strategy need not be held to expiry — exit into post-event strength or adjust; firefighting beats stubbornness.

## Strategies
- **Put back spread**: sell 1x ATM+100 put; buy 2x ATM-200 puts (Nifty ~13,600: sell 13,700PE, buy 2x 13,400PE)
- **Call back spread**: sell 1x ATM-100 call; buy 2x ATM+200 calls
- **Put ratio spread**: buy 1x ATM put (~Rs 175); sell 2x puts ~250-350 lower
- **Put butterfly (capped ratio)**: put ratio + buy 1x far OTM put (12,800)
- **Call ratio spread**: buy 1x ATM call; sell 2x calls 200 higher; optional far OTM call for cap
