## Core lessons
1. Markets bottom after big panic selling (capitulation); overreaction to one name often marks a multi-week low.
2. Panic in one bond/stock drags the whole sector down with it - contagion is mechanical, not fundamental.
3. Traders who understand irrationality can profit during it; accumulation often starts at maximum pessimism.
4. Markets price the lead indicator (next few months) - current bad news is already in the price (efficient market: you are last to know).
5. Very good news invites profit booking; very bad news invites policy rescue - so both can be sell/buy signals respectively.
6. Consensus is dangerous: when every retail message is bearish, the short trade is already crowded.
7. Defensive sectors (FMCG/IT/pharma) can rally through market-worst periods - index-level gloom hides stock-level bull markets.
8. In high-VIX environments most traders lose on both sides; option buying demands more skill than selling.
