## Key Lessons
- Implied volatility is almost always higher than historical volatility - paying full straddle premium around events is st
- Across 10 budgets, Nifty never closed more than 2% from the prior close except the COVID-coinciding 2020 budget (-3.26%)
- Even across seven major crises (Lehman, Greece, taper tantrum, China devaluation, demonetization+Trump, Brexit, Feb-2018
- Back spreads cut max loss by a third versus long straddles and win big only on large moves in one direction.
- If worried about uncapped ratio-spread risk, buy one far OTM option and convert to a butterfly.
- In a butterfly, buys and sells must balance (1-5-4) — only then is risk limited and margin benefits apply.
- Anchor strategy legs to the stock's all-time high: profit zone below the ATH, limited loss above it.
- A strategy need not be held to expiry — exit into post-event strength or adjust; firefighting beats stubbornness.
- Butterflies cap downside loss if the stock crashes, but upside loss beyond the upper strike is real money — hedge or man
- Long-term, non-directional trades with wide breakevens can out-return fast weekly decay trades.
- When the trend flips, close the old view - a bullish combo closed at +8% funded the bearish book.
- Run double ratios (call ratio + put ratio) when direction is unknown; margin ~Rs 1.5L for ~13% in a month.
- Review every posted trade publicly - wins and losses - to build trust.
- Complimentary trades: if first trade goes wrong, the second trade gives more profit
- After a very long consolidation, Bitcoin is likely to break out higher
