## Key Lessons
- Rising rates often signal a strong economy - 2003-07 delivered a seven-fold Sensex rise amid steady hikes.
- Markets fear the pace and motive of rate rises (withdrawal of support) more than the level.
- Low rates push spending and asset prices up; hikes redirect money to deposits and cool demand.
- Every macro fact has two valid readings - position size for being wrong, not conviction for being right.
- Tapering withdraws the exact liquidity that inflated emerging markets - the 2013 Nifty −20% template.
- Rates rising from growth are benign; rates rising from withdrawal of support are the danger.
