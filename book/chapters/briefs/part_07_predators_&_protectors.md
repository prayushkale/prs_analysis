### Verified Facts (Anchor Points)
- **Event 1 (Election Day, June 4, 2024)**: Nifty fell ~8.5% intraday, then recovered 32 record highs by Sept 2024. Use to illustrate:
    - How market‑wide circuit breakers did **not** trigger (because the fall was not fast enough to hit the 10% threshold in a single trade).
    - How SEBI’s dynamic margin system absorbed the volatility without systemic failure.
    - The role of institutional buying (FPIs, DIIs) in the subsequent recovery.
- **Event 2 (HDFC Bank, Jan 2024)**: Single‑stock circuit limit of 10% (now 20% for some) was hit – explain how stock‑level circuit breakers work and why they were tightened after the Covid crash.

### Enhancement Tasks
- **Table**: SEBI’s key regulatory actions post‑2020 (e.g., F&O entry barriers, increased margins, ASM framework). Include date, measure, and intended effect.
- **Diagram**: Circuit breaker triggering sequence – how market‑wide and stock‑level circuits interact.
- **Worked example**: Calculate margin required for a hypothetical Nifty futures position before/after the 2024 margin reforms – show impact on leverage.

### Complexity Estimate
**Easy‑Medium** – regulatory details need structuring but are conceptually straightforward.

### Length Target
**2,600 words**
