## Key Lessons (by frequency)
- [1×] May is historically a weak month for equities ('Sell in May') - factor seasonality into OI positioning.
- [1×] Known-bad data (zero auto sales) is priced in; markets react to surprises, not confirmations.
- [1×] Markets often bottom after a day of extreme volatility combined with a double bottom.
- [1×] Nifty top-50 average return 1994-2020 did not double in 6 years, underperforming risk-free debt instruments doubling in ~6.
- [1×] Index survivorship inflates returns: failing companies are ejected, so you average only the winning horses.
- [1×] Stock market suits only stock-pickers or those who time entries and exits; passive buy-and-hold is sold as a myth.
- [1×] Don't be a bull or a bear - be a chameleon; markets change trend when everyone leans one way.
- [1×] Always trade with the trend; fighting it with leverage (F&O) makes losses heavy.

## Strategy Setups
- **Sector entry discipline (SIP vs lumpsum)**: Long-term (3-5yr) investors: SIP into top IT names (Infosys, TCS, Wipro); momentum investors wait for dust to settle; lumpsum avoi
  - Risk: structural decline vs cyclical trough is unknowable in advance; SIP mitigates ti
  - Best regime: capitulation phase of a structurally hit sector
- **Put-selling ladder in collapsing-VIX rebound**: Sold puts across strikes from ~6,100 to 9,000 during April 2020 recovery
  - Risk: windfall not repeatable; VIX normalizes; requires conviction view
  - Best regime: post-crash VIX collapse with market refusing to make new lows
- **Debt-to-equity transfer ladder**: Park 100% in debt/liquid funds; move 20% to equity (Value-20 ETF or stocks) when market already corrected 10-15%; repeat per furth
  - Risk: Corrections can exceed the ladder's assumptions
  - Best regime: Correcting market of uncertain depth
