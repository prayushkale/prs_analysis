## Core lessons
1. Markets bottom after big panic selling (capitulation); overreaction to one name often marks a multi-week low.
2. Panic in one bond/stock drags the whole sector down with it - contagion is mechanical, not fundamental.
3. Traders who understand irrationality can profit during it; accumulation often starts at maximum pessimism.
4. High collapsing VIX plus a clear directional view is where option sellers make windfall profits - not repeatable monthly
5. Know when to exit: he closed nearly all positions after the Fibonacci target hit
6. Markets factor in known bad news (efficient market) - shorting after visible bad news is how traders get squeezed
7. A trader can profit in a falling market and lose in a rising one - the view matters, not the market's direction
8. Commodity businesses get PE ~10-11; telecom/retail get 40-150+ - business-mix change drives re-rating more than earnings alone.
