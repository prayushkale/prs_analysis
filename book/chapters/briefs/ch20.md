## Key Lessons
- Announced headline stimulus and actual cash outlay can differ by an order of magnitude - trade the market's verdict, not
- A break of a psychologically important round-number support after a disappointment signals continuation, not opportunity
- Divided US government stalls fiscal stimulus and keeps global markets uncertain; unified government (with VP tie-break) 
- A single pending election can be the hinge on which an extended bull market's next leg swings.
- Corporate results are the only core driver; monsoon, elections and policy are enabling factors.
- Reliance re-rated from an 8-10 PE commodity stock toward telecom/retail multiples, and much of that re-rating is already
