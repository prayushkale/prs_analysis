## Core lessons
1. Enter earnings trades the day before results and exit once the event resolves; hold further only with a stop-loss.
2. Finance protective wings by selling double quantity of farther strikes so the hedge costs nothing from pocket.
3. A naked short straddle is high risk; the same view becomes limited-risk when converted to an iron fly.
4. Stop-loss is non-negotiable: hit it and you can recover; blow up and you cannot (slow poison vs quick poison)
5. Square-off vs counter-strategy is decided by two questions: do you have additional capital and additional skill?
6. The 50% cash-equivalent requirement applies at broker level; brokers may pass it to clients (Zerodha does)
7. Cash equivalents: FD, Liquid BeES, liquid funds; Bharat Bond (~5% haircut) may qualify with some brokers
8. Run many small strategies across instruments so a rescue in one can be funded by closing another

## Strategies
- **Infosys earnings iron fly with financed wings**: Sell 650CE + 650PE (5 lots); buy 720CE + 580PE; sell 2x 750CE (10 lots) and 2x 550PE to finance the wings
- **Futures-plus-collateral long-term long**: Buy 1 lot long futures; park notional value in FD/Bharat Bond; pledge for collateral margin
- **Futures + rolling short straddle on a long-term holding**: Long monthly future; sell ATM calls; convert to straddles; roll puts lower as market falls (1460->1440); add 1520C
- **Hedged short put**: Sell near put + buy a further-OTM put same expiry
- **Hedged put sale (index)**: Sell 1600 put, buy 1500 put (June expiry); Bank Nifty call ladder 21500/22000/22500/23000 with weekly hedges
