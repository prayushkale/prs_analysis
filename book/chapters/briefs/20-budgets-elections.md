## Core lessons
1. Announced headline stimulus and actual cash outlay can differ by an order of magnitude - trade the market's verdict, not the headline.
2. A break of a psychologically important round-number support after a disappointment signals continuation, not opportunity.
3. Divided US government stalls fiscal stimulus and keeps global markets uncertain; unified government (with VP tie-break) can unlock a fresh rally leg.
4. A single pending election can be the hinge on which an extended bull market's next leg swings.
5. Corporate results are the only core driver; monsoon, elections and policy are enabling factors.
6. Reliance re-rated from an 8-10 PE commodity stock toward telecom/retail multiples, and much of that re-rating is already priced after +400%.
7. Bull-market corrections are fast and furious - two days can erase weeks.
8. Best pre-event posture for the unskilled: be flat, watch, re-enter after; enter early only if you can adjust positions under pressure.
