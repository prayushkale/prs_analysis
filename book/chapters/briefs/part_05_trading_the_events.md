# Compiler Brief: Part – "Trading the Events" (Chapters 19, 20, 21)

**Part Overview**  
This part equips traders with frameworks to anticipate, react to, and profit from high-impact market events – political, corporate, and regulatory. Each chapter dissects a distinct event category using real Indian market episodes, emphasizing pre-event positioning, intraday tactics, and post-event trend analysis.

---

## Chapter 19: Trading Political Events – The Election Shock

### 1. Purpose
Teach how to trade around unexpected political outcomes (e.g., election results) – from pre-event hedging to intraday volatility capture and post-event recovery plays.

### 2. Key Sources
- **0002** – Pre-election positioning strategies and option premiums.
- **0244** – Intraday minute-by-minute Nifty movement on June 4, 2024.
- **1534** – Post-election coalition dynamics and sectoral recovery (TDP/JDU support).
- **0657** – Risk management during black-swan political events.
- **1094** – Comparison of 2024 election vs. 2019 election trading patterns.

### 3. Verified Facts
- On June 4, 2024 (Lok Sabha counting day), Nifty fell ~8.5% intraday to ~21,281, closing ~-5.9% to -6% as BJP-led NDA looked short of majority.
- That night, TDP’s Chandrababu Naidu and JDU’s Nitish Kumar pledged support, forming the NDA government.
- Nifty recovered over subsequent months, scaling record highs 32 times in 2024, reaching ~26,000 only in late September 2024 (not same day).

### 4. Enhancement Tasks
- **Worked example**: Simulate a trader’s P&L using pre-election straddles vs. post-event futures.
- **Table**: Intraday Nifty price ladder (10:00 AM to 3:30 PM) with volume spikes.
- **Checklist**: Pre-event hedging checklist (options, sector rotation, cash position).

### 5. Complexity Estimate
Medium – requires understanding of options and event-driven volatility.

### 6. Length Target
3,500–4,000 words.

## Chapter 20: Trading Corporate Events – Earnings Surprises & Bank Results

### 1. Purpose
Demonstrate how to trade around corporate earnings announcements, focusing on bank results (e.g., HDFC Bank) – including pre-announcement positioning, reaction to surprises, and post-result drift.

### 2. Key Sources
- **0815** – HDFC Bank Q3 FY24 results analysis and market reaction.
- **0589** – Sectoral impact of bank earnings on Nifty Bank index.
- **2974** – Options strategies for earnings (straddles, strangles).
- **0041** – Post-result price discovery and institutional flows.
- **1616** – Case study: worst day since COVID crash (Jan 16, 2024).

### 3. Verified Facts
- On January 16, 2024, HDFC Bank shares plunged 8.5% – worst day since COVID crash – after Q3 results disappointed.
- Investors lost ~₹1 lakh crore in market cap that day.
- The event triggered the sharpest single-day fall for Nifty and Sensex since June 2022.
- Combined with US Fed signals, markets fell sharply in 19 months.

### 4. Enhancement Tasks
- **Worked example**: Calculate implied vs. realized volatility for HDFC Bank options pre- and post-result.
- **Table**: Comparison of HDFC Bank’s earnings surprise magnitude vs. price reaction (last 5 quarters).
- **Risk rule**: Position sizing for single-stock event risk (e.g., max 5% portfolio exposure).

### 5. Complexity Estimate
Medium – requires familiarity with earnings season dynamics and option Greeks.

### 6. Length Target
3,000–3,500 words.

### 3. Verified Facts
