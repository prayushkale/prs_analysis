- A break of a psychologically important round-number support after a disappointment signals continuation, not opportunity

- Bull-market corrections are fast and furious - two days can erase weeks.

- Strong macro (GST collections, credit growth) reduces tax-hike pressure on households.
- Markets know everything before you do - unexplained persistent selling is itself information.
