## Core lessons
1. Compute FII long/short percentages from NSE participant-wise open-interest reports instead of relying on TV sound bites.
2. Extreme FII index-short positioning (~85%) historically marks bottoms; read index and stock futures together for the true picture.
3. Option sellers pledge debt funds/FDs/liquid funds as collateral instead of paying cash margin
4. Exchange collateral haircuts can jump to 100% overnight - pledged positions then force-liquidate
5. Whatever happens anywhere in the world, the stock market feels it first
6. Success needs two things: an edge (view/skill) and a hedge.
7. Sell far OTM calls to finance a directional long - no out-of-pocket premium, capped upside is the accepted cost.
8. A short straddle in a quiet regime can yield high-single-digit monthly returns on margin.

## Strategies
- **Call ratio spread (financed directional)**: Long 1x 9400CE; short 2x 9800CE; short 1x 10000CE (300/600/300 qty), plus weekly far-OTM call sales to offset net debit
- **Hedged put sale (index)**: Sell 1600 put, buy 1500 put (June expiry); Bank Nifty call ladder 21500/22000/22500/23000 with weekly hedges
- **Dividend capture via cash-futures arbitrage**: Buy stock in cash market before ex-date; short equal futures; collect dividend; square both legs post ex-date
- **Nifty long / Bank Nifty short pair**: Long 1 lot Nifty futures + short 1 lot Bank Nifty futures (25 lot size), rebalanced views weekly
- **Short-stock rescue ladder (firefighting)**: Short future + sold call went wrong; rescue = sold puts at 4,500/4,700/4,800/4,900/5,000/5,200 (4,500 qty total) + long
