## Core lessons
1. Rising rates often signal a strong economy - 2003-07 delivered a seven-fold Sensex rise amid steady hikes.
2. Markets fear the pace and motive of rate rises (withdrawal of support) more than the level.
3. Low rates push spending and asset prices up; hikes redirect money to deposits and cool demand.
4. Every macro fact has two valid readings - position size for being wrong, not conviction for being right.
5. Tapering withdraws the exact liquidity that inflated emerging markets - the 2013 Nifty −20% template.
6. Rates rising from growth are benign; rates rising from withdrawal of support are the danger.
7. Long-term investors can ignore tantrums; traders must respect them.
8. A speculator is literally a watchman on the border - vigilance is the job description.
