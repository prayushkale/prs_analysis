## Core lessons
1. Rolling long futures costs ~5-6% a year in premium; long-term positions belong in cash, shorts earn the premium.
2. Stock lending & borrowing loses to futures shorting: no futures premium, borrowing cost, and full option margin on the hedge.
3. Market-wide position limit above 95% bans a stock; the ban persists until it falls below 80% - even on expiry day.
4. Margin money is calibrated to cover ~99% of moves: 2 short Nifty lots lose only ~35-40K in a 500-point gap against 1L+ margin.
5. Corrections are -10 to -20%, bear territory beyond -20% - label drawdowns by definition, not by fear.
6. India VIX at multi-year lows is the most dangerous regime for pure option sellers - one VIX spike erases months of theta.
7. In low VIX, always pair buys with sells so a volatility spike cushions both legs.
8. Sell the in-the-money strike and hedge in a far month, not the adjacent month, when running put calendars.
