## Key lessons (10)
- Prices move on results versus expectations, not on absolute results - better-than-feared can rally
- On expiry day, stocks can move opposite to results as shorts cover
- Never copy someone's strategy blindly unless you have their capital and skills (bus-pothole analogy)
- Without extra capital and adjustment skills, your only tool is a stop-loss in your mind
- Look at everything - news, flows, regulation - never one indicator alone
- When a known buying window opens, front-runners buy in cash and short futures; late chart-followers get trapped
- Order-entry errors can crash markets 15% in minutes and reverse; stop-losses get hit both ways
- If one system reliably worked, everyone would follow it and it would stop working
