# COMPILER BRIEF: Part – "The Trader's Mind" (Chapters 1, 2, 3)

## Overview
This part explores the psychological and behavioural dimensions of trading in Indian equity markets. It must equip readers with self-awareness of emotional triggers, cognitive biases, and practical mental frameworks to navigate volatility, news-driven swings, and long-term uncertainty. The three chapters build from understanding market-wide emotional cycles, through individual biases, to building a disciplined, resilient trading mindset.
