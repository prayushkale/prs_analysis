# Chapter 10: The Greeks, Demystified

## Core Lessons

1. *"Delta = change in option price per 1-point move in the underlying: ATM ~0.5, deep ITM ~1, far OTM near 0."*

2. *"Crypto options: short straddle works when Bitcoin consolidates - $250 margin for $115 profit in 1 week"*

3. *"Option prices never move by delta alone - gamma, theta, vega and rho bend real-world moves (ATM call gained 45 on a 114-point rally, not the expected ~57)."*

4. *"After a very long consolidation, Bitcoin is likely to break out higher"*

5. *"High interest-rate economies carry higher option premiums (rho), but rates move too slowly to matter daily."*

6. *"Deep-ITM options (delta ~1) behave like a synthetic future."*

7. *"Crypto options on Indian exchanges: pay INR, receive INR, no foreign currency involved"*

8. *"Time value only decays: theta never works for the buyer, always for the seller."*

9. *"Method evolution: Greeks-aware, always-hedged, directional-first became the mature teaching stance by mid-2023."*

10. *"Call deltas run 0 to +1; put deltas 0 to -1."*

11. *"Theta is small far from expiry and explodes in the final week — decay is non-linear."*

12. *"Complimentary trades: if first trade goes wrong, the second trade gives more profit"*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Theta-aware expiry choice

**Construction:** Buyers: options with ≥1 month left; sellers: write near expiries while managing gamma

**Numbers:** ₹100 option loses ~₹2/day at 50 days, ~₹50/day at 2 days (all else equal)

**Best used when:** Seller: final expiry weeks; buyer: far-dated directional plays

**Adjustments:** Buyers roll to next month rather than hold through final-week decay

**Risks:** Near-expiry gamma can overwhelm theta for both sides on news shocks


### Bitcoin Short Straddle

**Construction:** Sell BTC 91,000 call + sell 91,000 put

**Numbers:** Margin ~$250; profit $115 in 1 week on 10-week expiry

**Best used when:** Consolidation/range-bound Bitcoin

**Adjustments:** Close when 50% profit achieved

**Risks:** Unlimited risk if Bitcoin breaks out violently


### BTC Short Straddle

**Construction:** Sell BTC 91,000 call and put

**Numbers:** Margin $250; profit >100% in 1 week

**Best used when:** Consolidation $81,000-$95,000

**Adjustments:** Close at 50% profit

**Risks:** Unlimited risk


### BTC Put Ratio

**Construction:** Buy 1 put, sell 2 lower puts

**Numbers:** Complimentary to short straddle

**Best used when:** When first trade is at risk

**Adjustments:** N/A

**Risks:** Limited


### BTC Call Butterfly

**Construction:** Buy 100K CE, sell 2x 105K/110K/115K, buy 5x 126K CE

**Numbers:** Hedge ~$1,200-12,000

**Best used when:** Range-bound with slight bullish bias

**Adjustments:** N/A

**Risks:** Limited risk design


## Key Takeaways

- Delta = change in option price per 1-point move in the underlying: ATM ~0.5, deep ITM ~1, far OTM near 0.
- Crypto options: short straddle works when Bitcoin consolidates - $250 margin for $115 profit in 1 week
- Option prices never move by delta alone - gamma, theta, vega and rho bend real-world moves (ATM call gained 45 on a 114-point rally, not the expected ~57).
- After a very long consolidation, Bitcoin is likely to break out higher
- High interest-rate economies carry higher option premiums (rho), but rates move too slowly to matter daily.
- Deep-ITM options (delta ~1) behave like a synthetic future.
