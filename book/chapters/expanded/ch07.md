# Chapter 7: Microstructure: Gaps, Expiries and the Last Half Hour

## Core Lessons

1. *"Ceasefire credit-claiming by third parties is noise for markets; what matters is whether capital treats the region as investable."*

2. *"A single mistyped order can crash the index 15% in seconds before exchange intervention."*

3. *"Index options settle in cash - the retail-friendly structure; stock options carry compulsory physical delivery."*

4. *"Military conflicts must be treated as military conflicts; converting them into religious or political wars only deepens them."*

5. *"Spread and covered-call legs mutually offset physical delivery only when both legs finish in the money."*

6. *"Always square up stock-option positions yourself before ban/deadline; the exchange's auction fallback can cost ~20% premiums."*

7. *"Government agencies never resolve matters by phone — they issue notices; Gmail sender IDs and urgency are scam tells."*

8. *"Trade indices, not stocks: ITM stock options that lapse trigger physical delivery; index options are cash-settled."*

9. *"Brokers escalate margins into expiry week (stepwise to full contract value) and square off non-compliant positions."*

10. *"One weekly expiry per exchange would remove the daily manipulation window that daily expiries created."*

11. *"If you cannot explain what happens to premium when you exit, you are not ready to trade options."*

12. *"Exchange circuit limits revise upward only after being hit; the gap between 'hit' and 'revised' is where freak fills happen."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### IndusInd earnings-day iron-fly style combo

**Construction:** Sell 400CE + sell 400PE, buy 500CE + buy 300PE (current month)

**Numbers:** profit down 77% vs expected 95% fall; stock closed ~15-17% higher

**Best used when:** earnings day when heavy short positioning built up

**Adjustments:** speaker's own adjustments not disclosed; states adjustments depend on additional capital

**Risks:** big gap moves on expiry; adjustments need reserve capital


### Hedged short put

**Construction:** Sell near put + buy a further-OTM put same expiry

**Numbers:** Speaker example: sold 9400-put style positions; hedge put cuts margin drastically and caps loss

**Best used when:** Any short-put view; mandatory insurance

**Adjustments:** Not advised live; know risk-reward before entry

**Risks:** Unhedged version: margin cascade and uncapped gap risk


### Expiry-day naked call selling (documented failure)

**Construction:** Sell 10,000 CE on weekly expiry day (18 June 2020) in size at Rs 4

**Numbers:** Entry ~Rs 4, stop-loss exit ~Rs 23, loss ~Rs 2.82 lakh

**Best used when:** High-probability decay days only, with strict size

**Adjustments:** Stop-loss enforced

**Risks:** Low-premium options have violent multi-fold spikes; this is the canonical blowup pattern


### Gamma awareness for expiry-week selling

**Construction:** Short ATM options held into expiry vs square-off rule

**Numbers:** ATM premium 50 → ~110 (more than doubled) on a 100-point sudden move; normal delta move would be 50→60

**Best used when:** Never for sellers in the final days unless hedged

**Adjustments:** Close/roll before expiry week; keep far-OTM hedges as real hedges, not margin dummies

**Risks:** Whipsaw doubles short-premium exposure in hours
