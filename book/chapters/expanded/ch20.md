# Chapter 20: Budgets and Elections: Case Files

## Core Lessons

1. *"Stock market wants a stable government, not necessarily a specific party - the 2004-2008 bull run happened under a coalition government"*

2. *"Derive year targets from measurable anchors (VIX-implied move, FD risk premium, rollover hurdle), not vibes."*

3. *"Finance Ministry uses media leaks to test market reaction to potential budget measures"*

4. *"A known election outcome is already in the price; only the losing scenario is a live risk."*

5. *"Adani stocks are treated as the high-beta proxy for a BJP win: outperform on either result direction."*

6. *"Selling calls, buying puts, and shorting futures are all short exposure - an upside black swan hits all three."*

7. *"Stock picking matters more than market direction: five years can give zero return (Maruti, Hero MotoCorp) before payback"*

8. *"Adjustment skill - not prediction - determines survival when tail events strike either way."*

9. *"High personal tax vs low corporate tax pushes wealth into company structures, formalizing the economy."*

10. *"Best pre-event posture for the unskilled: be flat, watch, re-enter after; enter early only if you can adjust positions under pressure."*

11. *"After a major shock resolves, the following year tends to deliver outsized returns - markets front-run recovery by 6-12 months."*

12. *"The rally phases: 15% in 2 months (Nov-Dec), consolidation, then 10%+ in 4 months after election clarity"*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Budget-day posture rule

**Construction:** close positions before Feb 1 unless skilled in adjustments

**Numbers:** Bank Nifty fell 1,800 points in two days of profit booking; Nifty 14,660->14,250->14,780 swings

**Best used when:** before binary events

**Adjustments:** skilled traders may hold and adjust

**Risks:** event gap against the position


### Long-dated short straddle + wings (position disclosed)

**Construction:** Sold December-expiry 19,000 and 18,000 short straddles; sold 16,000 put and 20,000 call as wings

**Numbers:** profit driven by falling India VIX and range-bound tape

**Best used when:** declining VIX, sideways-to-down market

**Adjustments:** not stated

**Risks:** big directional move breaks the straddle; wings cap tail loss


### High-dividend-yield safety screen

**Construction:** Buy stocks with very high dividend yield (examples cited: Tata Investment ~5.5-6%, Rane group ~10-11%, Coal India) as downside-protected equity

**Numbers:** PFC ~90-100 -> ~270-280 (+300% incl. ~10% yield); REC ~120 -> ~220 plus 1:3 bonus

**Best used when:** market lows / value phases

**Adjustments:** hold for dividend if price falls; no forced selling

**Risks:** sector structural risk (power discoms); dividend tax changes


### Election pair bet (Adani vs Nifty)

**Construction:** Friend: long Adani Enterprises futures + short Nifty futures; speaker: short Adani Ent + long Nifty; contract sizes equalized

**Numbers:** Speaker's estimate: BJP-comfortable-win path — Nifty +~5%, Adani Ent +~10%

**Best used when:** Around binary political events when two parties hold opposite high-conviction views

**Adjustments:** None discussed; closed after result clarity

**Risks:** Beta mismatch between Adani and Nifty; gap risk on result day; correlation breakdown


### Post-correction long call

**Construction:** Buy ATM call option with 6-12 month expiry when Nifty corrects >10%

**Numbers:** Nifty 22,000→26,000 rally after June 2024 election crash; 500-600 point recovery

**Best used when:** After a 10%+ correction from peak

**Adjustments:** Book profit when Nifty recovers 500+ points

**Risks:** Time decay if market stays flat
