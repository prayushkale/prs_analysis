# Chapter 19: The Event Playbook

## Core Lessons

1. *"Known, pre-announced policy changes are already in prices - only surprises move markets, so tax-hike headlines rarely crash a market that expected them."*

2. *"Divided US government stalls fiscal stimulus and keeps global markets uncertain; unified government (with VP tie-break) can unlock a fresh rally leg."*

3. *"Surprise, not the event, moves markets: expected outcomes produce 100-150 point moves; surprises produce 20-30% moves."*

4. *"Event habituation applies to all repeated shocks (same bad news, second time, is a non-event)."*

5. *"Central banks price their announcements: bitter medicine is served with sugar - taper came bundled with a no-rate-hike pledge and equities rallied."*

6. *"Exit near legs once the event resolves; keep far wings only with a stop-loss."*

7. *"A 20% drop from highs is a bear market by definition - check which markets are actually in one before panicking."*

8. *"Enter earnings trades the day before results and exit once the event resolves; hold further only with a stop-loss."*

9. *"Going flat into binary policy events (budget + central bank same day) is a legitimate position."*

10. *"Pre-budget rumors move markets even when 95% never materialize - price the fear, not the fact."*

11. *"If upside to consensus target is <10% but surprise downside is 20-30%, defer lumpsum investing; continue SIPs."*

12. *"IV rises for far expiries when uncertainty persists and collapses once the outcome is known."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Infosys earnings iron fly with financed wings

**Construction:** Sell 650CE + 650PE (5 lots); buy 720CE + 580PE; sell 2x 750CE (10 lots) and 2x 550PE to finance the wings

**Numbers:** 650CE @31, 650PE @27.50, 720CE @7.35, 580PE @8.40, 750CE @4, 550PE @4.46; max profit ~Rs.3.58 lakh (~15%) if Infosys closes at 650; protection 710 up / 590 down

**Best used when:** Results eve with elevated implied volatility

**Adjustments:** Exit after results or keep far wings with a stop-loss; watch volatility crush

**Risks:** Beyond 750/550 the financing legs turn the risk unlimited again; overnight gap risk

**Best used when:** Immediately after results, when volatility crush pays all sold legs

### 6-month bearish combo (retrospective)

**Construction:** Buy 1x 26,000 put (Mar series) + sell 2x 24,000 puts + sell calls, initiated Oct 31 with Nifty ~25,000

**Numbers:** margin ~2.2-2.5 crore; profit ~1.23 crore (~50% in 6 months, ~8%/month); long put +81 lakh, short 24,000 puts +99.6 lakh, short call also profitable
