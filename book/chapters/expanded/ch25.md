# Chapter 25: 2025–2026: Tariffs, Jane Street and the New Regime

## Core Lessons

1. *"On event days the close matters, not the open - an opening capitulation can still be an aberration."*

2. *"In a locally-driven correction, the Lehman/COVID 'next-year +100%' logic fails - those were global resets, this is policy-driven."*

3. *"Known risks are not surprises: hedge in advance, because hedges bought after the gap are unaffordable"*

4. *"Define the technical trigger, then trade only on closing-basis confirmation — intraday breaches mislead both ways."*

5. *"Weak bulls being thrown out is a precondition for a sustainable rally"*

6. *"A sector index can enter a bear market while the headline index holds — weightage hides divergence."*

7. *"Broad-based rallies with VIX falling mark genuine stabilization; large-cap-only rallies are just defense."*

8. *"Markets either correct in price or in time — a valuation reset can mean years of sideways."*

9. *"Unverified market-moving news released during hours lets manipulators profit from option positions - positional traders pay the price."*

10. *"Domestic policy changes (like gold-loan norms) can trigger sector-wide de-risking without any global trigger."*

11. *"Never react in the first 15-30 minutes of a gap-down; book shorts into panic and sell calls into the 150-200 point recovery."*

12. *"A flat open in a trending bear market is the most dangerous open: trends extend when nobody is forced to react"*


## Key Takeaways

- On event days the close matters, not the open - an opening capitulation can still be an aberration.
- In a locally-driven correction, the Lehman/COVID 'next-year +100%' logic fails - those were global resets, this is policy-driven.
- Known risks are not surprises: hedge in advance, because hedges bought after the gap are unaffordable
- Define the technical trigger, then trade only on closing-basis confirmation — intraday breaches mislead both ways.
- Weak bulls being thrown out is a precondition for a sustainable rally
- A sector index can enter a bear market while the headline index holds — weightage hides divergence.
