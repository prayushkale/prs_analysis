# Chapter 29: Investing vs Trading: Choosing Your Game

## Core Lessons

1. *"Park idle capital in debt funds and redeploy 20% tranches into equity as markets get cheaper."*

2. *"A durable, dominant media franchise can trade at a fraction of peer multiples when saddled with a legal/political overhang - the discount itself is the opportunity if the overhang clears."*

3. *"Nifty top-50 average return 1994-2020 did not double in 6 years, underperforming risk-free debt instruments doubling in ~6."*

4. *"Contrarians win long-term; momentum target-setting is a different game - know which one you are playing."*

5. *"In the WSJ decade-long dartboard contest, pros beat the darts 61% of the time but beat the Dow only 51% of the time."*

6. *"When a mid/small-cap index has rallied >100%, start systematic withdrawal (SWP) - the next two years historically go nowhere or down."*

7. *"India's collateral-haircut paradox: risky equity ETF gets 5% haircut while government-debt Liquid BeES gets 10% - know the rules before pledging."*

8. *"If everyone knows an outcome, it is priced in; expected wins move markets 1-2%, surprises move them 20%."*

9. *"Listed bond ETFs double as high-quality collateral (5% haircut), making idle capital productive for trading."*

10. *"Smallcaps tend to outperform largecaps in bull markets."*

11. *"Direct real estate suffers illiquidity discounts; unitized trusts trade daily like stocks."*

12. *"A 10–11% dividend yield creates its own floor: at half the price the yield doubles to 20–25%, drawing buyers before catastrophe."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### FD/collateral-backed premium recycling

**Construction:** ~25 lakh FD pledged; premiums from option selling recycled to absorb small booked losses without touching the FD

**Numbers:** Example: 25 lakh FD collateral (as stated)

**Best used when:** Option sellers running multiple small premium streams

**Adjustments:** Net small booked losses against premium inflows from other positions

**Risks:** If booked losses exceed total premium received, cash injection is mandatory


### Staggered index investing with cash-secured puts

**Construction:** SIP into a Nifty 50 ETF; optionally place cash-secured puts at desired lower entries

**Numbers:** Nifty PE ~16 (May 2020); historical buy zone 15-16, sell zone 28-30; Dow 1929 crash ~90%; 1982-2000 bull ~15x

**Best used when:** Post-crash pessimism; long horizon

**Adjustments:** Add on further 5-10% falls

**Risks:** Multi-year drawdowns; requires no-liquidity-needs capital


### Bharat Bond as trading collateral

**Construction:** Buy Bharat Bond ETF units; pledge to broker as collateral for F&O margins

**Numbers:** ~7.6% indicative 10yr yield; 20% LTCG with indexation >3yr; 5% haircut; EBBETF 0423 ~1019, EBBETF 0430 ~1050 (May 2020)

**Best used when:** declining interest rates; idle capital awaiting trade opportunities

**Adjustments:** n/a

**Risks:** rising rates compress price; credit concentration in PSU issuers


### Liquid BeES cash-component collateral

**Construction:** Buy Liquid BeES; pledge as the cash component of margin

**Numbers:** price ~1,000 +/- 1 paisa; interest via additional units monthly; no STT/stamp duty

**Best used when:** any time idle capital sits in a trading account

**Adjustments:** n/a

**Risks:** haircut 10%; liquidity depth


### Covered call on a long-term holding (mentioned method)

**Construction:** Buy shares (in lot-size multiples), sell ATM/slightly OTM calls each cycle

**Numbers:** not specified beyond 'reduce my cost price'

**Best used when:** sideways-to-mildly-bullish phases for a stock you want to own long term

**Adjustments:** roll calls as stock moves

**Risks:** upside capped; stock can enter F&O ban period, making hedging harder (noted for this stock)
