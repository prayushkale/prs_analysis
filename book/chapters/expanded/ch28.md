# Chapter 28: Taxes: The Trader's Real Opponent

## Core Lessons

1. *"QIPs at huge oversubscription signal institutional demand and often predate strength in the stock."*

2. *"File by the due date to carry forward losses - belated returns forfeit set-off."*

3. *"Incidental expenses (data, advisory, depreciation, proportionate rent) are deductible if directly related to trading and not paid in cash."*

4. *"Own exactly one self-occupied home regardless of financial math; treat additional properties purely as investments."*

5. *"Compete only with your own past self; benchmarking against rivals distracts from process."*

6. *"Market income splits into three heads: intraday speculative, equity capital gains (15% STCG / 10% LTCG beyond 1 lakh), and F&O as business income."*

7. *"STT was created in lieu of LTCG tax; when LTCG returned, keeping both became double taxation with no deduction allowed."*

8. *"Treat residents at least on par with NRIs/FPIs given GIFT City concessions; transaction-cost asymmetry pushes volume offshore."*

9. *"Rationalizing 5/12/18/28 slabs to essentially 5/18 moves most 28% goods (ACs, refrigerators, washing machines) to 18% - a direct ~10-point consumption stimulus that lifted auto stocks ~4-5% to all-time highs the next session."*

10. *"File the loss year return anyway: carried-forward losses offset future profits; unfiled losses are gone forever."*

11. *"NRI sending money to India for property needs impeccable documentation or it becomes taxable income"*

12. *"ITRs cannot be filed retroactively; file every year even with nil tax - returns are needed for loans and visas."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Collateral-funded option selling (HNI pattern)

**Construction:** Pledge debt mutual funds/bank FDs as collateral; sell options against the margin; keep interest as primary return

**Numbers:** Stated pattern: 6-7% interest on debt funds while trading on pledged limit

**Best used when:** Wealthy, income-focused sellers who accept trading as a bonus stream

**Adjustments:** Not applicable - structural allocation

**Risks:** Trading losses eat the collateral cushion; interest is not guaranteed against credit events


### Reverse arbitrage on futures discount

**Construction:** Sell physical shares held in demat, buy the discounted futures; converge at expiry and reverse both

**Numbers:** Example: stock 1000 spot vs 990 futures captures ~10 points

**Best used when:** Futures at significant discount (e.g., Nifty futures 80-90 points under spot)

**Adjustments:** Hold to convergence

**Risks:** Only works if shares already held; capital locked; convergence timing


### Covered call via futures leg (margin-optimized)

**Construction:** Hold long futures instead of physical shares; sell calls against it

**Numbers:** Stated: span margin zero for the covered call against long futures; exposure margin ~2% index, ~3.5% stock options

**Best used when:** Large holders wanting option income without physical-settlement complexity

**Adjustments:** Roll calls up as futures rally

**Risks:** Futures MTM swings; margin calls if exposure margin rules change


### Collateral ladder for option sellers

**Construction:** Pledge Liquid BeES/debt funds (~90% collateral value); keep a small cash buffer for debit legs

**Numbers:** Buy-side premium debits settle immediately; sell-side credits arrive next day - keep cash for combos

**Best used when:** All option sellers using collateral

**Adjustments:** Top up cash before multi-leg strategies

**Risks:** Settlement timing on multi-leg entries; penalty on shortfall


### Collateral-first capital structure

**Construction:** buy Bharat Bond ETF -> pledge to broker -> trade on limit; profits recycled into equity on corrections

**Numbers:** Bharat Bond ~6.95% p.a.; FD ~6% (top banks 5-5.5%); post-tax ~6.5% vs ~3%; haircut 6% vs 12-99% stocks; price dipped ~2% post-budget

**Best used when:** any serious option seller; capital > Rs 5 lakh

**Adjustments:** rotate debt into equities at correction lows

**Risks:** interest-rate/credit risk on the bond; 50% cash rule at brokers who pass it to clients
