# Chapter 8: Why Derivatives Exist

## Core Lessons

1. *"In India, derivatives turnover is 9x the cash market; Nifty is the world's most-traded derivative contract"*

2. *"Cumulative P&L transparency across months builds trust: show losers too (June -1.2L)."*

3. *"Bank Nifty short straddle plus active adjustments produced the majority of monthly profit; futures were used only for hedging, not speculation."*

4. *"The five major derivative instruments: forwards, futures, options, swaps, swaptions."*

5. *"Margin, not conviction, is usually the binding constraint in adjustment trading."*

6. *"Charges apply on premium (about 30,000) instead of contract value (about 7.5 lakh) - roughly 25x lower cost base."*

7. *"Transaction costs are paid in wins and losses alike — at high volume they become the largest certain expense."*

8. *"Accept small planned losses inside the ladder: the 1700 protective put lost over 1 lakh while the overall program stayed heavily profitable."*

9. *"Futures MTM losses must be paid in cash; synthetic-option MTM can rest against collateral until booked."*

10. *"Earnings straddle rule: sell both call and put just before results, exit both the next day - the credit survives the surprise."*

11. *"Hedging a directional position with short calls can outperform the stock itself - profit doubled versus holding the future alone."*

12. *"Futures premium compression (150→60 points) is a real-time gauge of fading upside conviction."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Futures-plus-collateral long-term long

**Construction:** Buy 1 lot long futures; park notional value in FD/Bharat Bond; pledge for collateral margin

**Numbers:** Reliance example: ~7.5L notional; premium ~Rs 5/share/month (~30k/yr); interest ~50k/yr at 7-7.5%; net saving ~10-20k/yr; incremental short-call margin ~50k vs 1.75L for cash holder

**Best used when:** Large capital, long-term bullish, willing to manage monthly rollovers

**Adjustments:** Sell one OTM call monthly against the long for income; exit stock/futures on strength if assigned economics improve

**Risks:** Monthly rollover discipline; gap/MTM risk on the futures leg; lot-size minimum locks ~7.5L capital


### Futures + rolling short straddle on a long-term holding

**Construction:** Long monthly future; sell ATM calls; convert to straddles; roll puts lower as market falls (1460->1440); add 1520C

**Numbers:** entry 1422, May-2020 series; +159,186 total vs ~35-40k unhedged; margin <=5 lakh vs 15 lakh cash

**Best used when:** long-term bullish holding with two-way volatility

**Adjustments:** square and re-strike puts on reversals; roll future at ~1 point cost in bearish tape

**Risks:** gap risk on naked legs; assignment/roll slippage


### Synthetic futures (long/short)

**Construction:** Long: buy ATM call + sell ATM put (same expiry). Short: reverse legs

**Numbers:** Demo: June futures -222 vs synthetic -219 on a 222-point fall; cost base 7.5 lakh (futures) vs ~30,000 (synthetic) per lot at Nifty 10,000, lot 75

**Best used when:** Any directional view where collateral efficiency matters; far-month exposure

**Adjustments:** Exit both legs together; unbalanced exit leaves a naked leg with higher margin

**Risks:** Stock synthetics fail on deep-ITM liquidity; index only


### Hedged long stock/futures with call ladder + straddle conversion

**Construction:** Long 2 lots (1000 shares) Reliance futures from ~1010; sold 1580/1600/1640/1680/1700 calls progressively; converted a 1500 short call into a short straddle by selling 1500 puts (later doubled after error)

**Numbers:** June: stock +~3,00,000; options overlay +~2,90,000; total 592,592 at month-end week; ~30 trades executed

**Best used when:** Strongly trending large-cap you want to hold; sell calls above trend to monetize drift

**Adjustments:** Rolled sold calls up the ladder as price ran; doubled puts after leg error; kept net long exposure

**Risks:** Gap above highest sold call caps upside; margin spikes when doubling corrective legs


### Bank Nifty short straddle with adjustments

**Construction:** Short straddle at 21,500 strike (July 2020 series), rolled/adjusted as market moved higher

**Numbers:** ~19.85 lakh profit on the straddle program in July; account capital ~95 lakh

**Best used when:** High-IV, trending month where adjustments can ride the move

**Adjustments:** Continuous rolling and re-striking as Bank Nifty trended; described as 'too many trades' to itemize

**Risks:** Adjustment churn requires margin headroom and discipline; unprotected gap risk on the naked side


## Key Takeaways

- In India, derivatives turnover is 9x the cash market; Nifty is the world's most-traded derivative contract
- Cumulative P&L transparency across months builds trust: show losers too (June -1.2L).
- Bank Nifty short straddle plus active adjustments produced the majority of monthly profit; futures were used only for hedging, not speculation.
- The five major derivative instruments: forwards, futures, options, swaps, swaptions.
- Margin, not conviction, is usually the binding constraint in adjustment trading.
- Charges apply on premium (about 30,000) instead of contract value (about 7.5 lakh) - roughly 25x lower cost base.
