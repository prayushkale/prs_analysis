# Chapter 5: Volatility Is a Regime, Not a Number

## Core Lessons

1. *"Bull runs start with a dip-then-launch pattern; a right-curve dip is fuel, not failure"*

2. *"Fear can compound: VIX at 31 in 2008/2020 still went to 85-86; 'buy fear' needs a timing overlay, not just a level."*

3. *"Far out-of-the-money strikes carry higher IV because tiny premiums make every point move a large percentage gain."*

4. *"Do not buy naked options when VIX is falling; run structures that own a short premium leg."*

5. *"Implied volatility is the market's forecast of near-term movement: high IV expects big moves, low IV expects quiet ones."*

6. *"A VIX of 15 means the market prices a ~15% move - use implied move, not gut feel, for annual ranges."*

7. *"FII selling of ~1 billion dollars/week no longer crashes the market because retail + mutual funds absorb it."*

8. *"Position-level losses (up to ~1.75 lakh single positions) coexist with net profit - judge the portfolio, not each leg."*

9. *"In low VIX, always pair buys with sells so a volatility spike cushions both legs."*

10. *"India VIX at multi-year lows is the most dangerous regime for pure option sellers - one VIX spike erases months of theta."*

11. *"November-February is the best window to initiate long-dated option structures."*

12. *"India VIX is a one-month instrument - it cannot see IV built for events six months out; check the term structure yourself."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### VIX-based expected-move strangle placement

**Construction:** Sell puts below spot - (VIX/sqrt(12))% and calls above spot + (VIX/sqrt(12))%

**Numbers:** VIX 60 -> monthly band ~17.3%; spot 8,000 -> sell puts below ~6,500, calls above ~9,500; May 8,000 straddle ~1,600 (~20% for 37 trading days)

**Best used when:** Elevated VIX (crisis); recompute daily as VIX moves

**Adjustments:** None stated - guideline framing, refresh band as VIX changes

**Risks:** Band is statistical, not a wall; theta/other factors shift premiums; violent moves can exceed the band


### Falling-VIX combo (call ratio + put ratio)

**Construction:** Bought 8,200CE, sold 3x 9,000CE; bought 8,000PE, sold 7,400PE + 7,300PE (as stated)

**Numbers:** Sold ~3x quantity on the far leg both sides

**Best used when:** Falling VIX with a directional view; benefits from vega collapse on the short legs

**Adjustments:** Not stated

**Risks:** Upside loss above 9,000 on the 3:1 call ratio; downside acceleration through 7,300 on the put ratio


### Strike-and-expiry put ladder (illustrative, high risk)

**Construction:** Sold puts at 6,500 / 7,000 / 7,300 / 7,400 / 7,500 / 7,800 / 8,500 across weekly, May and June expiries; small stock-option positions (IndusInd 800 qty @22.52; ~2,300PE on a NBFC stock, 4 lots @220)

**Numbers:** Capital ~95 lakh -> ~45 lakh profit in 6 sessions (~50%); sold Jun 7,000PE @475, May 6,500PE @210 (closed 80), May 7,000PE @324 (closed 117)

**Best used when:** Extreme high VIX with stabilizing market (COVID April 2020)

**Adjustments:** Laddered entries across expiries instead of single-strike concentration

**Risks:** Aggressive directional sizing; a renewed crash through the ladder strikes would produce large losses; explicitly not replicable by retail


### IndusInd Bank earnings hedged straddle (May series)

**Construction:** Sell May 400CE @62 + 400PE @52; buy current-month 500CE @4.25 + 300PE @5.25 as protection

**Numbers:** Net premium ~Rs.94.55/share = Rs.37,800 per lot; margin ~Rs.60,000; >50% return if IndusInd closes near 400 at May expiry

**Best used when:** High-IV stock ahead of results; next-month premium rich relative to risk

**Adjustments:** Exit opportunity after the results; avoid holding the full month

**Risks:** Very large move beyond the wings; one month is a long time in trading


### Hedged put sale (index)

**Construction:** Sell 1600 put, buy 1500 put (June expiry); Bank Nifty call ladder 21500/22000/22500/23000 with weekly hedges

**Numbers:** FII context: -6000 crore in 4 sessions; VIX 30+

**Best used when:** High-VIX range with defined support

**Adjustments:** Weekly hedge rolls to cut margin

**Risks:** Range break below support
