# Chapter 1: Why Most Traders Lose

## Core Lessons

1. *"People look for certainty in an uncertain market - that search itself is the mistake."*

2. *"Due diligence cannot protect against a good actor turning bad later; risk remains with the investor."*

3. *"When the 10% correction finally comes, you will not dare to buy; investors need patience or hedges, not exits."*

4. *"Never benchmark yourself against the richest investors - comparison is an obsession, not a goal."*

5. *"Selective exits matter - close only the endangered side (long or short, or one symbol) rather than dumping everything."*

6. *"Traders buy strength and sell weakness; investors must take the opposite side."*

7. *"Keep business decisions and generosity in separate mental accounts - mixing them damages both."*

8. *"Benchmark the decision: full-time trading must beat (trading return minus delegation cost) plus your salary - else keep the job and trade long-term options part-time."*

9. *"The government's biggest gold lesson is its own: selling Sovereign Gold Bonds unhedged at 2,500-2,700/gram against a price near 10,000 is a naked short position gone wrong."*

10. *"Gamblers exit broke, but new gamblers keep arriving — that cycle, not the instrument, is the harm."*

11. *"Roll futures monthly; keep the hedge structure intact across series."*

12. *"When good news (rate cuts, OMO, swaps) fails to lift a market, something structural is wrong - don't average down on hope."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Cash Secured Put

**Construction:** Sell a put at the strike where you want to own the stock; keep the full cash ready for delivery

**Numbers:** Bajaj Finance 1800PE sold at Rs.100 (2 lots, Rs.50,000 collected); Nifty Apr 7500PE ~Rs.57; Dec-2020 6000PE ~Rs.200; ~Rs.19 lakh premium collected; claimed Rs.45 lakh FY21 P&L in 3 weeks

**Best used when:** Sideways-to-up markets, or when you want a lower entry price in a quality stock

**Adjustments:** Square off the put and buy shares in the open market instead of physical delivery; exit any time before expiry

**Risks:** Profit capped at premium; stock can fall far below strike; requires full cash backing of the strike


### Put-selling ladder in collapsing-VIX rebound

**Construction:** Sold puts across strikes from ~6,100 to 9,000 during April 2020 recovery

**Numbers:** April profit 66,30,478 on ~95L capital; VIX 80-85 -> 55-60 -> ~40; June 9000PE sold at 500, closed 180

**Best used when:** post-crash VIX collapse with market refusing to make new lows

**Adjustments:** not detailed in this episode

**Risks:** windfall not repeatable; VIX normalizes; requires conviction view


### Strike-selection framework (ITM vs ATM vs OTM)

**Construction:** Same directional view expressed via 12,500CE / 13,000CE / 13,500CE with spot ~13,000

**Numbers:** premiums 600 / 275 / 75; costs ~45,000 / ~22,000 / ~5,000 per lot (lot 75); profits at 13,400 expiry: +300 / +100 / 0; losses at 12,500: -600 / -300 / -75

**Best used when:** ITM for high-probability moderate moves; OTM only for large fast moves

**Adjustments:** exit anytime; premium decays

**Risks:** OTM total loss most probable; ITM larger absolute rupee loss


### Pledged-corpus income engine (personal-finance structure)

**Construction:** Rs 2 crore into Bharat Bond (~7%); pledge for ~Rs 1.8 crore F&O limit; trade 2 liquid large-caps with simple short strangles/straddles only

**Numbers:** ~49.5 lakh booked profit over ~3 months plus ~5 lakh MTM; car ~1.62 crore ex-showroom plus ~40-45 lakh taxes; loan ~7% matched by bond yield

**Best used when:** When one can genuinely generate 30-40% p.a. and wants a purchase without draining principal

**Adjustments:** Monthly P&L directed to EMI; MTM buffer kept for several EMIs

**Risks:** Trading losses still leave the loan to repay; pledged collateral can lose value; concentration in two stocks


### Nifty/Bank Nifty put ladders into budget

**Construction:** Weekly puts 17,000-16,600 (Nifty); monthly 16,500-16,000; Bank Nifty 36,500-36,000 (all sold)

**Numbers:** Far-OTM premiums 20-134 rupees; ~2,500 rupees per 100 captured vs ~1-1.5 lakh margin (~2.5% in 2-3 sessions)

**Best used when:** Bullish-to-neutral into an event after a correction, with global VIX subdued

**Adjustments:** Hedge sold at 38,300 put for the positional book; no stops on far ladders

**Risks:** Genuine crash blows through all sold strikes
