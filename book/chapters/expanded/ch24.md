# Chapter 24: 2023–2024: The Bull Run and Its First Crack

## Core Lessons

1. *"Election-catalyst trends can run far past overbought — 1,000 points in 10 sessions"*

2. *"Don't fight the regulators (Raghuram Rajan's 'don't fight the central bank')"*

3. *"Government tax nudges like a windfall-tax cut are sentiment signals worth watching."*

4. *"Incremental reserve requirements hit banks instantly but fade from memory in days - policy micro-tools move Bank Nifty far more than the repo headline."*

5. *"Support and resistance matter mainly to options players, who build strategies around them"*

6. *"In an uptrend, selling streaks usually exhaust in about three days"*

7. *"Long-dated options turn time decay into an ally during chaos - the only calm position in a rout"*

8. *"DIIs buying more than FIIs sell does not guarantee support - absorption fails when the marginal buyer disappears."*

9. *"Privileged exit-poll access flows into charts before results - common traders must rely on technicals."*

10. *"India is a liquidity-driven market, not a data-driven market - bad GDP barely dents it"*

11. *"When far-month premiums balloon while near-term VIX is calm, the market is pricing a big future event"*

12. *"VIX direction on a red day separates correction from reversal: falling VIX + deflating calls = healthy correction"*


## Key Takeaways

- Election-catalyst trends can run far past overbought — 1,000 points in 10 sessions
- Don't fight the regulators (Raghuram Rajan's 'don't fight the central bank')
- Government tax nudges like a windfall-tax cut are sentiment signals worth watching.
- Incremental reserve requirements hit banks instantly but fade from memory in days - policy micro-tools move Bank Nifty far more than the repo headline.
- Support and resistance matter mainly to options players, who build strategies around them
- In an uptrend, selling streaks usually exhaust in about three days
