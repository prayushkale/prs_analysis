# Chapter 9: Calls and Puts

## Core Lessons

1. *"An option is asymmetric betting: buyer risks only the premium; seller pays 1:1 per point beyond the strike."*

2. *"Event-delayed stocks (BPCL divestment) offer corrected entry points when the catalyst is deferred, not dead."*

3. *"Option buyer: limited risk (premium), theoretically unlimited reward."*

4. *"Instead of long futures, buy an in-the-money call; instead of short futures, buy an in-the-money put - similar payoff, strictly capped downside."*

5. *"No knowledge means risk in every market segment, not just options - bankruptcies happened in plain equities too."*

6. *"Selling a call is likewise not buying a put; the asymmetry is identical on the bearish side."*

7. *"ITM put calendar: sell near-month put (collect premium), buy far-month put (protection) - best for budget when you expect near-term consolidation but want long-term protection"*

8. *"Contra investing buys the worst-performing sector of a crash year and waits for mean reversion."*

9. *"Income stacking: dividend + monthly OTM call selling + modest appreciation can compound to ~18%+."*

10. *"Across 25 years of Indo-Pak crises Nifty fell roughly 4-6% each time; use the base rate, but remember coincident crises (dotcom, Lehman) distort attribution."*

11. *"Selling a put profits in 4 of 5 scenarios (~80%); buying a call profits in 1 of 5 (~20%) - the structural seller's edge."*

12. *"Short-term directional trades in cash market cost ~10x the charges of futures (~Rs1,684 vs Rs174 on a Rs7.35L position)."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Cash-secured puts (ICICI 300PE, HDFC 840PE)

**Construction:** Sell puts below market with full cash set aside for delivery

**Numbers:** +33,000 ICICI and +28,000 HDFC in May 2020

**Best used when:** moderately bullish with stocks near support

**Adjustments:** take delivery if assigned; roll if view changes

**Risks:** sharp breakdown below strike forces delivery at loss


### Put ratio spread (9100PE bought / 8500PE sold)

**Construction:** Long lower put, short further-OTM puts

**Numbers:** +26,000 (May 2020)

**Best used when:** mildly bearish to flat

**Adjustments:** close short side on breakdown

**Risks:** accelerated loss beyond short strikes


### Synthetic long future

**Construction:** Buy ATM call + sell ATM put (same strike/expiry)

**Numbers:** Worked day (8 Jul 2020): Nifty 10766 to 10682 (-82); 10800 CE fell 36, PE rose 44, synthetic loss ~80 vs future 82-84

**Best used when:** Directional long exposure with lower capital than futures; premium received on the put offsets

**Adjustments:** Roll at new ATM as trend extends

**Risks:** Early assignment is absent in India (European index options); margin on the short put leg


### Put-selling vs call-buying scenario framework

**Construction:** ATM put sold at premium P vs ATM call bought at premium C (Nifty 10,750; 30-Jul-2020 expiry)

**Numbers:** Put sold at 212; call bought at 160; put seller loses only below 10,538; call buyer profits only above 10,910

**Best used when:** Teaching framework for choosing selling over buying under a mild directional view

**Adjustments:** n/a (conceptual)

**Risks:** Tail risk: seller's loss is open-ended beyond breakeven


### Cash-secured put entry on BPCL

**Construction:** Sold BPCL 350PE at ~14 (August 2020 expiry), cash reserved for assignment

**Numbers:** Collect 14/share/month; effective entry 336 if assigned

**Best used when:** Stock corrected on event delay; willing to own at strike

**Adjustments:** Roll or take delivery and switch to covered calls

**Risks:** Assignment in extended divestment delay / crude spike
