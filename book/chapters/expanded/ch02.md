# Chapter 2: The Anatomy of a Trader's Failure

## Core Lessons

1. *"Being 87% short, FIIs are the fuel for any violent squeeze - positioning extremes predict the next move's direction, not its timing."*

2. *"Do not trade on flash news planted to help someone exit - the Buffett-Kotak rumor is the cautionary case."*

3. *"Consecutive 200-point intraday falls typically end in a violent recovery once institutions step in"*

4. *"SIP on expiry day beats SIP on the 5th because expiry-day closes skew low in volatile series."*

5. *"Weekend policy U-turns are binary risk: never carry full size into an unresolved crisis"*

6. *"Nobody starts a restaurant with a predetermined stop-loss - diagnose, adjust, and only quit when it is genuinely not your cup of tea."*

7. *"Strong global macro plus local FII selling is a lag, not a ledger - India typically catches up violently"*

8. *"A series closing at its low statistically argues for an early next-series bounce; failure of that bounce is itself information."*

9. *"Exchanges owe transparency on resume timing; brokers' auto-square-off turns a pause into real losses."*

10. *"Consolidation after a vertical rally is healthy - run, rest, resume."*

11. *"Stock-specific chaos (Adani) can hold an index hostage while everything else is calm; position for the range, not the headline."*

12. *"Future premium ~100 pts vs the normal 40-50 must normalize - expect either spot pop or premium decay."*
