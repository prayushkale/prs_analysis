# Chapter 14: Controlled Direction: Spreads & Ratio Spreads

## Core Lessons

1. *"Buying 1 and selling 2 at a higher strike creates a near-zero-cost position with capped downside"*

2. *"Consensus is dangerous: when 99% expect a fall, markets climb the wall of worry."*

3. *"A crash portfolio: ~60% index ETFs, ~10% gold (crisis hedge — gold rose in 2008 and 2020), ~30% debt/REITs you can liquidate to buy the dip."*

4. *"Effective margin = gross margin minus next-day premium credit - check net, not gross, returns."*

5. *"Exchange 20% bonuses are trading margin, not withdrawable cash - they are clawed back as you profit."*

6. *"New crypto venues suffer thin liquidity and wide bid-ask impact cost until participation grows."*

7. *"Combine a call ratio (buy 1, sell 2 far) with a cash-secured put: no downside loss in a wide band, uncapped-ish profit in the middle."*

8. *"Percentage returns look big on low-margin structures; judge risk by the capped loss, not the headline return."*

9. *"Two supertrend lines (20-day/5-day) proxy monthly and weekly trends; alignment defines regime."*

10. *"In thin crypto options markets, quoted spreads mean orders chase prices; build slippage into any planned structure"*

11. *"Far out-of-the-money strikes carry double demand (hedgers plus margin-conscious sellers), so selling them captures richer premium."*

12. *"The money-making process should be boring, not exciting - slow positions beat daily thrill trades."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### ICICI Bank call ratio (1x2) for earnings

**Construction:** Buy 1 lot 350CE at ~2,440; sell 2 lots 380CE at ~1,190

**Numbers:** near-zero net cost (~40-50 paise paid); max loss downside ~825; max profit zone 350-380; unlimited risk above 410; margin ~1.5 lakh net (2.53 lakh naked 380CE)

**Best used when:** mildly bullish stock pinned below known resistance into results

**Adjustments:** not shown in this episode; speaker flags adjustment need if it gaps above 410

**Risks:** gap above the sold strike doubles exposure; earnings gaps both ways


### Limited-risk call ratio spread (1:3:2)

**Construction:** Buy 1x 10,500 CE (Aug), sell 3x 11,000 CE (Aug), buy 2x 11,500 CE (Aug)

**Numbers:** Net credit ~Rs 1,900; margin ~Rs 65,000 (Rs 63,000 effective); max profit ~Rs 37,000 at Nifty 11,000; upper breakeven 11,253; max loss ~Rs 37,000 above 11,500

**Best used when:** After a sharp rally, into strong resistance (200-DMA + 50% retracement of 12,400->7,500)

**Adjustments:** Exit all legs together; partial exit leaves naked short calls

**Risks:** Sustained breakout above 11,500 hits max loss; requires two-month view


### ITC pre-earnings call ratio with put floor

**Construction:** Buy 2 lots 197.5CE @6.55; sell 4 lots 205CE @3.50; sell 2 lots 185PE @1.05 (July 2020 expiry)

**Numbers:** Max profit ~Rs 50,000 near 205; loss begins beyond 212.5 (upper breakeven ~214 after put leg); margin ~Rs 2.31-2.5 lakh (Opstra showed 41k - wrong); underlying 3-month high 208

**Best used when:** Mildly bullish stock in a range into results, with high dividend yield cushioning the downside

**Adjustments:** Take delivery below 185 (long-term buy thesis); adjust/firefight on a breakout above 212.5

**Risks:** Gap above the 3:2 short-call ratio (225+) after results; assignment/obligation on short leg


### Range-filtered PCR

**Construction:** Sum put OI and call OI only for strikes within expected monthly range (spot +/-1,000 early in month, +/-700-800 mid-month); PCR = put OI / call OI

**Numbers:** Dec 2020: raw PCR 1.81 vs filtered 1.12 (put 1.67 cr, call 1.49 cr in 12,800-14,200)

**Best used when:** Weekly/mid-month sentiment read before taking directional or option-selling positions

**Adjustments:** Recompute as the expected range narrows through the month

**Risks:** Range guess can be wrong in trending/volatile months; still an OI-based sentiment proxy, not a timing signal


### Put back spread

**Construction:** sell 1x ATM+100 put; buy 2x ATM-200 puts (Nifty ~13,600: sell 13,700PE, buy 2x 13,400PE)

**Numbers:** lower breakeven ~13,253 vs straddle's 13,162; max loss ~Rs 20,000 vs straddle ~Rs 32,000; margin ~Rs 1 lakh

**Best used when:** bearish into event; profits only on large falls

**Adjustments:** roll or convert with far put

**Risks:** sideways market loses the debit; margin understated by platforms
