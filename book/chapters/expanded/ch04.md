# Chapter 4: The Engine Room: FII/DII Flows

## Core Lessons

1. *"Retail 'shock absorber' buying has limits - most post-2020 entrants have never seen a real bear market."*

2. *"Options volume spikes after losses: bigger traders become sellers, smaller ones become buyers - both compounding the 90%-lose statistics"*

3. *"Daily commentary automatically turns bullish on up days and bearish on down days - judge markets on longer time frames."*

4. *"Compare like-for-like windows when judging 'expensive' markets: earnings growth can offset index gains (midcap +67% vs EPS +66% argument)."*

5. *"Euphoria markers: record IPO supply with instant doubling, retail denial on profits, institutions buying highs they used to sell."*

6. *"Crypto sizing rule: 5-10% of capital max; NRI/tax-residency rules apply since crypto is outside SEBI purview."*

7. *"Institutional selling driven by policy hostility ends with a government concession - watch tax-policy news as the release valve."*

8. *"Mean-reversion parity: if the index gives zero return while FDs compound, subsequent bull legs historically over-compensate to restore equity premium."*

9. *"A correct macro thesis can still lose money when government intervention breaks the transmission (crude-down OMC trade)."*

10. *"Markets rise when retail+domestic buying absorbs FII selling; the balance breaking is the real bear signal"*

11. *"FIIs face a double whammy - falling prices plus falling rupee - which is why they sell first in a crisis."*

12. *"Equity returns are non-linear: five flat years (Maruti, Hero MotoCorp, ITC) can precede a single year that pays for all of them"*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Call ratio spread (financed directional)

**Construction:** Long 1x 9400CE; short 2x 9800CE; short 1x 10000CE (300/600/300 qty), plus weekly far-OTM call sales to offset net debit

**Numbers:** 9400CE ~219; 9800CE ~80; 10000CE ~45; net ~-4300 before weekly sales (~+2800 after); max profit ~1,15,000 at 9800 expiry; loss begins ~10,080

**Best used when:** Mildly bullish, expect stay below written strikes

**Adjustments:** Promised follow-up through expiry; weekly-call sales used to neutralize debit

**Risks:** Unlimited above 10,080 (net short top strike); upside capped at 9800 zone


### Dividend capture via cash-futures arbitrage

**Construction:** Buy stock in cash market before ex-date; short equal futures; collect dividend; square both legs post ex-date

**Numbers:** ITC dividend >10 rupees on a ~200 rupee stock; ~5% return in days as stated

**Best used when:** Extraordinary dividends (>5% of market value) where futures still price the gross dividend

**Adjustments:** Square up after ex-date convergence

**Risks:** Futures may already price the dividend; margin on short futures; execution costs


### Long-dated no-loss call ratio (Dec-2022)

**Construction:** Buy 18,000CE; sell 2x 20,000CE — premium received equals premium paid

**Numbers:** Profit zone 18,000-22,000; zero downside loss even if Nifty → 0; upside risk beyond 22,000 managed by rolling

**Best used when:** Moderately bullish long-term view with capped upside expectation

**Adjustments:** Roll short calls higher in stages if Nifty approaches 22,000

**Risks:** Opportunity cost and upside whipsaw beyond 22,000; wide-spread liquidity in far expiries


### Long-dated Nifty short strangle (annual range)

**Construction:** Sold December-2022 15,000 put and 21,000 call

**Numbers:** Combined premium ~700 points (~35,000 per lot); margin ~1.8 lakh initially, easing to 80-90k later; already ~100 points in profit at recording

**Best used when:** Wide VIX-implied annual range with no intent to predict direction

**Adjustments:** None needed unless a boundary approaches; long-dated structure ignores short-term news

**Risks:** Both sides breached in an extreme year; margin lock-up for 12 months


### Deep OTM put selling on bullish structural view

**Construction:** Sell December 15,000 and 16,000 Nifty puts as the stated position while maintaining buy-on-dips view

**Numbers:** Entry after 2,000-point rally from 15,200 low

**Best used when:** Post-capitulation, structure turned bullish, 200-DMA reclaimed

**Adjustments:** Buy on dips; exit on unforeseen shocks

**Risks:** A new black-swan shock (COVID-style) invalidates the view; speaker explicitly flags unforeseen circumstances

- Crypto sizing rule: 5-10% of capital max; NRI/tax-residency rules apply since crypto is outside SEBI purview.
