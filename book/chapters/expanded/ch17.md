# Chapter 17: Portfolio Protection: Hedges That Actually Hedge

## Core Lessons

1. *"India taxes F&O profits in the year earned but cash-equity losses only offset cash gains for 8 years - hedged portfolios can owe tax exceeding net profit."*

2. *"A stable rupee removes gold's currency-hedge argument for Indian investors."*

3. *"India runs European-style options; early-exercise assignment would make hedged books unmanageable overnight."*

4. *"Stop the bleeding first, treat later: when a position is gapping against you, cap the tail before planning the recovery."*

5. *"Every ~2 years a crisis hits markets (Lehman 2008, Greece 2011, taper 2013, China 2015, demonetization 2016, Brexit 2017, COVID 2020) yet long-term India story stayed intact."*

6. *"Firefighting playbook: sell puts into an adverse spike at successively higher supports, scale quantity as levels hold, cap tail with far calls."*

7. *"What matters is how much capital you can handle, not percentage ROI; a collateral-backed seller's ROI is 'infinite'."*

8. *"Long-short equity (long the stronger, short the weaker) profits in both directions if the spread thesis holds."*

9. *"A 1%-per-month worst-case outcome plus collateral yield still compounds meaningfully - judge trades at portfolio level."*

10. *"Momentum investing studies WHY a stock already ran up and whether the driver persists - not blind chasing."*

11. *"The managed-account grey area exists because licensed vehicles are unviable; giving someone trading password is at your own risk."*

12. *"Odds markets convert money flows into implied probabilities: 1:1 payout = 50% implied - the market fears 50-50 election outcomes, not particular winners."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Collateral-funded option selling (framework)

**Construction:** Sell options against collateral: bank FD, shares, mutual funds or bank guarantee pledged to the broker

**Numbers:** Buyer pays 2,000-3,000 premium cash vs seller margin ~80,000-90,000 (as stated) but pledgable in kind

**Best used when:** Traders with large capital seeking steady returns

**Adjustments:** Losing short can be adjusted via short futures or selling calls on the other side

**Risks:** Margin calls on booked losses; losses can exceed premium received if unhedged


### Hedged short option (spread)

**Construction:** Sell 10,000C / buy 10,200C (200pt); variants 300-400pt

**Numbers:** ~25,000-32,000 margin vs 80-90k naked; max risk = spread width

**Best used when:** any directional credit view

**Adjustments:** roll wings as market trends

**Risks:** max loss = width x lot; premium give-up


### Short straddle with far wings

**Construction:** Sell 9,500CE+9,500PE (July), buy 8,500PE+10,500CE (June weekly/next series)

**Numbers:** margin ~45,000-55,000 after ~40-60k premium receivable; straddle premium >1,000 pts in July 2020

**Best used when:** high-IV months with distant wings affordable

**Adjustments:** re-buy expired wings weekly

**Risks:** gap through wings; weekly-wing rollover friction


### ITM put calendar

**Construction:** Sell 10,000PE current month, buy 10,000PE next month

**Numbers:** margin <30,000

**Best used when:** neutral-to-mildly-bearish

**Adjustments:** roll short leg at expiry

**Risks:** fast rallies inflate long-leg decay loss


### Infosys bearish iron-condor-style spread + firefighting ladder

**Construction:** Initial: 800PE long / 770PE short; 830CE short / 900CE long. Firefight: sold 780PE, 830PE, 900PE (later doubled), 850PE, 1000CE

**Numbers:** Potential loss ~Rs 1.6-1.7L; final +Rs 21,826; extra margin ~Rs 15L; Infosys 780->830->915 (+20% in 2 days)

**Best used when:** Adverse post-results spike in a stock you can sell puts on

**Adjustments:** Ladder put sales at rising supports; double quantity only as levels hold; cap with far-OTM calls

**Risks:** Continued vertical moves; margin exhaustion; doubling short puts is aggressive
