# Chapter 30: The Hard Assets Debate

## Core Lessons

1. *"Debt mutual funds pass corporate defaults straight to investors; DHFL and IL&FS shocks left several debt funds below FD returns."*

2. *"Low rates ignite the capex cycle: leverage expands EPS, bull markets expand the multiple - price = EPS x PE can double from both sides."*

3. *"Gold has rallied in bursts when central banks slash rates after crises (1980, 2008-13); the next cut cycle is the bull case."*

4. *"A cumulative bond's fair value grows by accreted interest; early exit means accepting the market price."*

5. *"REITs convert illiquid property into tradable, dividend-paying units — real-estate income without ₹100-crore tickets."*

6. *"Regional real estate tracks the economics of its buyers: Kerala property softened as Gulf oil economies stalled."*

7. *"Sometimes clickbait thumbnails can become real - in 2019, Bank Nifty fell 1,000 points minutes before Nirmala Sitharaman was appointed FM"*

8. *"Hold at least ~10% of a portfolio in gold as a rate-cycle hedge."*

9. *"Shorting borrowed bonds costs interest foregone plus borrowing cost (~10-11%), limiting short-seller profit in credit instruments"*

10. *"Bond math is the transmission channel from central-bank policy to equity valuations."*

11. *"Short sellers attack via whatever instruments exist offshore: bonds, ADRs, offshore derivatives"*

12. *"When everybody is bearish on rising prices, that is the bull case: sentiment extremes precede trend continuation."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Long-dated index option buying on IV collapse

**Construction:** Deployed 60-70 lakh across December 2020 Nifty options (puts and calls), holding 30-40 lakh cash for adjustments

**Numbers:** +~1 lakh/day for three consecutive days (~3% on deployed capital) as IV fell from ~30 to ~20

**Best used when:** Post-panic IV compression with market stabilization

**Adjustments:** Keep 30-40% dry powder for adjustments

**Risks:** Time decay if IV stays low and market ranges; long options bleed without direction


### Covered-bond retail allocation

**Construction:** Via retail covered-bond platform: minimum ~Rs10,000; pools collateralized by vehicle/gold/housing loan books held by a trustee

**Numbers:** Issue cited: 10.25% p.a., monthly payouts, ~23-month maturity, ~Rs20 crore pool; historical HNI entry was Rs10 lakh minimum

**Best used when:** Low-rate environment; investors wanting FD-beating yield with collateral protection

**Adjustments:** Ladder maturities across issues; prefer diverse collateral pools

**Risks:** Secondary liquidity not guaranteed - be able to hold full term; platform/issuer concentration
