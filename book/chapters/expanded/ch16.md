# Chapter 16: Butterflies & Condors

## Core Lessons

1. *"Call butterfly at lower end of range: limited risk, captures upside if breakout happens"*

2. *"Along-the-way management matters: expiry-date max loss is not the practical loss; a stop can exit the structure for a fraction of worst case."*

3. *"Whatever happens anywhere in the world, the stock market feels it first"*

4. *"Exchange collateral haircuts can jump to 100% overnight - pledged positions then force-liquidate"*

5. *"Option sellers pledge debt funds/FDs/liquid funds as collateral instead of paying cash margin"*

6. *"After a long consolidation, Bitcoin tends to break higher"*

7. *"Multi-leg entries via basket orders remove legging risk and execution hesitation - prepare strikes, quantities and side before the event day."*

8. *"Define maximum loss before the event: this structure capped loss at 1.6 lakh only in the extreme above-900 scenario, with no downside risk below 770."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Infosys earnings iron structure (put debit spread + call ratio credit wings)

**Construction:** Buy 800 PE (1 lot); sell 770 PE (1 lot); sell 830 CE (2 lots); buy 900 CE (2 lots)

**Numbers:** Max profit 43,000; max loss 1,60,000 (only if stock closes above 900 at expiry); probability ~71%; breakeven 833; net credit ~7,000

**Best used when:** Pre-earnings on a momentum stock near highs where a moderate move is likelier than a blowout

**Adjustments:** Stop-loss exit if stock runs through upper wing pre-event; roll after IV crush

**Risks:** Strong beat-and-raise above 900 creates the defined max loss; low liquidity in stock options


### BTC Call Butterfly

**Construction:** Buy 1x 100K CE, sell 2x 105K/110K/115K, buy 5x 126K CE

**Numbers:** Margin ~$360-$373; max loss stated ~$6.7k (verify)

**Best used when:** Lower end of consolidation range, expect breakout

**Adjustments:** Close if Bitcoin breaks below 80,000

**Risks:** Max loss is limited but significant


## Key Takeaways

- Call butterfly at lower end of range: limited risk, captures upside if breakout happens
- Along-the-way management matters: expiry-date max loss is not the practical loss; a stop can exit the structure for a fraction of worst case.
- Whatever happens anywhere in the world, the stock market feels it first
- Exchange collateral haircuts can jump to 100% overnight - pledged positions then force-liquidate
- Option sellers pledge debt funds/FDs/liquid funds as collateral instead of paying cash margin
- After a long consolidation, Bitcoin tends to break higher
