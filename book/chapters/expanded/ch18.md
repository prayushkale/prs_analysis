# Chapter 18: Firefighting: Rescue and Adjustment

## Core Lessons

1. *"Gambler's fallacy: past streaks don't change next-day probabilities - a 5-day fall can continue on day 6."*

2. *"Announce the risk of a trade before the event, then adjust mechanically after - not emotionally."*

3. *"When a thesis breaks, sell a further OTM option to convert the position into a net credit instead of accepting the planned loss."*

4. *"Stock options move violently; small-capital traders should stick to indices."*

5. *"The 50% cash-equivalent requirement applies at broker level; brokers may pass it to clients (Zerodha does)"*

6. *"Leverage shrinks margin (25x/50x/100x) but multiplies liquidation risk - choose the lowest workable leverage."*

7. *"Fund a long option by selling wider strikes so net cash outlay is near zero."*

8. *"Run paired slightly-bearish and slightly-bullish combos so one side profits whichever way volatility breaks."*

9. *"High-risk books need standby capital: after a 40% book drawdown, topping up preserved the profit position."*

10. *"Adjustments and firefighting (not stop-losses) are how a losing view becomes a small profit or small loss."*

11. *"Transfer technicalities matter: XRP deposits need tag/memo or funds get stranded."*

12. *"Converting a losing directional short into a hedged combo (long future + sold call + sold puts) can cap damage."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### OTM credit-sale adjustment on a losing ratio

**Construction:** Existing: long 350CE, short 2x 380CE (max loss ~0.50/share ~ Rs 800/lot). Adjustment: sell 1x 400CE at ~3.65

**Numbers:** Pre-adjustment loss ~Rs 3,000; post-adjustment profit ~Rs 2,000 per lot

**Best used when:** After a results miss confirms the directional view is dead

**Adjustments:** Collected credit exceeds the defined max loss; no further action needed unless stock reverses hard

**Risks:** New short call adds upside risk; acceptable because view is now neutral-bearish


### Short-stock rescue ladder (firefighting)

**Construction:** Short future + sold call went wrong; rescue = sold puts at 4,500/4,700/4,800/4,900/5,000/5,200 (4,500 qty total) + long Jan future + sold 5,500 call

**Numbers:** Stock +250 (4,900->5,150); drawdown 3.5-4 lakh; ~1 crore extra margin; ~10 lakh margin per 1,000 qty; result +1.49 lakh

**Best used when:** When a short directional view is hit by a gap/momentum move you believe is temporary

**Adjustments:** Ladder put sales into the spike, convert to hedged combo, keep averaging only with reserve capital

**Risks:** Requires massive reserve margin; if the rally continues, losses compound - works only with deep pockets and fast execution


### Bitcoin wide-breakeven combo (June)

**Construction:** Buy 1x BTC 35,000 call; sell 2x 50,000 calls; sell 1x 55,000 call; buy 1x 25,000 put; sell 2x 20,000 puts

**Numbers:** 35,000 call ~7,500 USD premium; margin ~1,500 USD at 25x leverage (750 at 50x, 375 at 100x); expiry breakevens ~15,000 and ~60,000; T+1 profitable zone ~20,000-38,000

**Best used when:** Post-crash stabilization with elevated IV; expecting a wide range, not a breakout

**Adjustments:** None detailed; monitor via what-if analysis

**Risks:** Move beyond 60,000 (or below 15,000) creates losses; thin crypto option liquidity widens spreads


### Paired BTC combos (bearish + bullish)

**Construction:** Bearish: buy 22,000PE (1000 qty), sell 25,000CE, 30,000CE, 15,000PE. Bullish: buy 20,000CE (~$2,800), sell 27,000CE x3 (~$600 each), sell 16,000PE x2 (~$450 each)

**Numbers:** Bullish combo net debit ~$100 (2800 paid vs 2700 collected); earlier bearish combo ~$1,000 profit at peak; BTC 19,000->21,500 in a week

**Best used when:** Wide two-sided uncertainty with high IV; define comfort band roughly 16,000-27,000

**Adjustments:** Sold additional 18,000/17,000/16,000 puts to offset the losing leg's drift; keep adjusting toward expiry

**Risks:** Loss if BTC breaks below ~16,000 or above ~27,000; crypto exchange counterparty risk; high IV means fast premium swings


## Key Takeaways

- Gambler's fallacy: past streaks don't change next-day probabilities - a 5-day fall can continue on day 6.
- Announce the risk of a trade before the event, then adjust mechanically after - not emotionally.
- When a thesis breaks, sell a further OTM option to convert the position into a net credit instead of accepting the planned loss.
- Stock options move violently; small-capital traders should stick to indices.
- The 50% cash-equivalent requirement applies at broker level; brokers may pass it to clients (Zerodha does)
- Leverage shrinks margin (25x/50x/100x) but multiplies liquidation risk - choose the lowest workable leverage.
