# Chapter 11: Implied Volatility: The Real Price of Options

## Core Lessons

1. *"Delta = change in option price per 1-point move in the underlying: ATM ~0.5, deep ITM ~1, far OTM near 0."*

2. *"Option premium behavior on flat sessions reveals directional bias before price does."*

3. *"When VIX falls, put premiums collapse while calls stay flat - premium behavior mirrors volatility, not direction."*

4. *"ITM options trading below intrinsic value signal forced liquidation and can be arbitraged, but size remains small for buyers."*

5. *"Far out-of-the-money strikes carry higher IV because tiny premiums make every point move a large percentage gain."*

6. *"Deep-ITM options (delta ~1) behave like a synthetic future."*

7. *"Implied volatility is the market's forecast of near-term movement: high IV expects big moves, low IV expects quiet ones."*

8. *"A VIX of 15 means the market prices a ~15% move - use implied move, not gut feel, for annual ranges."*

9. *"FII selling of ~1 billion dollars/week no longer crashes the market because retail + mutual funds absorb it."*

10. *"A 1:3 put ratio with breakeven 1,800 points below spot is a defined-risk way to hold a long-horizon hedge through an event."*

11. *"Derive year targets from measurable anchors (VIX-implied move, FD risk premium, rollover hurdle), not vibes."*

12. *"A known election outcome is already in the price; only the losing scenario is a live risk."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### IndusInd Bank earnings hedged straddle (May series)

**Construction:** Sell May 400CE @62 + 400PE @52; buy current-month 500CE @4.25 + 300PE @5.25 as protection

**Numbers:** Net premium ~Rs.94.55/share = Rs.37,800 per lot; margin ~Rs.60,000; >50% return if IndusInd closes near 400 at May expiry

**Best used when:** High-IV stock ahead of results; next-month premium rich relative to risk

**Adjustments:** Exit opportunity after the results; avoid holding the full month

**Risks:** Very large move beyond the wings; one month is a long time in trading


### News-pop short straddle

**Construction:** Sell ATM call + put same strike (2700, lot 300) into an IV spike

**Numbers:** IV 33→39, IV percentile 64→82; straddle +14 pts day one = ~₹4,000 on ~₹1.5L effective margin; target ₹8-10K by results IV crush

**Best used when:** Post-news elevated IV with binary event (results) next day

**Adjustments:** Stop-loss on combined premium; exit into post-event IV collapse

**Risks:** Gap through breakevens before crush; direction reversal


### Put back spread

**Construction:** sell 1x ATM+100 put; buy 2x ATM-200 puts (Nifty ~13,600: sell 13,700PE, buy 2x 13,400PE)

**Numbers:** lower breakeven ~13,253 vs straddle's 13,162; max loss ~Rs 20,000 vs straddle ~Rs 32,000; margin ~Rs 1 lakh

**Best used when:** bearish into event; profits only on large falls

**Adjustments:** roll or convert with far put

**Risks:** sideways market loses the debit; margin understated by platforms


### Call back spread

**Construction:** sell 1x ATM-100 call; buy 2x ATM+200 calls

**Numbers:** profit begins ~14,000 vs straddle 14,050; max loss ~Rs 20,000

**Best used when:** bullish into event

**Adjustments:** same mirror logic

**Risks:** small loss on big fall; sideways loses


### Put ratio spread

**Construction:** buy 1x ATM put (~Rs 175); sell 2x puts ~250-350 lower

**Numbers:** max profit ~Rs 18,000-19,000 near 13,300-13,350; loss begins below ~13,100 and is uncapped; upside loss ~Rs 1,000

**Best used when:** mildly bearish; expects limited fall

**Adjustments:** buy far OTM put to cap risk (butterfly)

**Risks:** crash below short strikes = uncapped loss


## Key Takeaways

- Delta = change in option price per 1-point move in the underlying: ATM ~0.5, deep ITM ~1, far OTM near 0.
- Option premium behavior on flat sessions reveals directional bias before price does.
- When VIX falls, put premiums collapse while calls stay flat - premium behavior mirrors volatility, not direction.
- ITM options trading below intrinsic value signal forced liquidation and can be arbitraged, but size remains small for buyers.
- Far out-of-the-money strikes carry higher IV because tiny premiums make every point move a large percentage gain.
- Deep-ITM options (delta ~1) behave like a synthetic future.
