# Chapter 21: Wars, Crashes and Black Swans

## Core Lessons

1. *"A falling market needs distance to stop, like a car braking - expect follow-through before reversal."*

2. *"The first casualty of war is truth; neither side's claims (5 jets vs denials) can be trusted - wait for international confirmation."*

3. *"Never predict direction immediately after results - let the position, not the narrative, settle."*

4. *"Corrections are -10 to -20%, bear territory beyond -20% - label drawdowns by definition, not by fear."*

5. *"FIIs selling ₹30,000-35,000 crore per month for 7 consecutive months is unprecedented"*

6. *"Big conflicts are usually resource conflicts - identify the underlying commodity (here natural gas), not the headline."*

7. *"Japan's market gave zero return for 40 years despite a good economy - the same could happen to India"*

8. *"Expect the unexpected: one keystroke error can wipe out everything; only trade full-time with capital you can survive losing entirely."*

9. *"After a very bad year (2008, 2020, 2022), the following year tends to deliver the best returns - position for recovery, not continuation."*

10. *"Six problems for Indian markets: rupee depreciation, crude oil, high valuation, competing assets, global money to semiconductors, and tax terrorism"*

11. *"Geopolitics moves sectors: tariff pressure can be negotiated into market-access concessions (Tesla entry) that upend incumbents."*

12. *"Never underestimate the enemy: a financially weak Pakistan still launched hundreds of drones per day."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Bank Nifty rollover analysis

**Construction:** Long (or short) futures rolled monthly

**Numbers:** ~100 pts typical next-month premium; ~1,200 pts (~5-6%) cost per year for longs; shorts collect it

**Best used when:** Long-term shorts benefit from rollover premium; long-term longs should buy cash instead

**Adjustments:** Hedging is cheaper on futures than on cash positions

**Risks:** Premium varies 40-200 pts monthly; longs underperform cash by the premium


### Warrant as long-term call substitute

**Construction:** Buy HDFC-W3 warrant at ~₹410; exercise anytime till Aug 2023 by paying ₹2,165 to receive 1 share (total cost ₹2,575); lot ~600 (max loss ~₹24,000)

**Numbers:** Issue ₹180 + strike ₹2,165 = ₹2,345 all-in at issue; market warrant ~₹400-410; HDFC spot ~2,300-2,400 after peaking near 3,000

**Best used when:** Bullish multi-month view on an FII-favourite stock when FIIs turn buyers

**Adjustments:** None - exercise or abandon

**Risks:** 100% premium loss if share stays below strike; poor liquidity; issuer credit risk
