# Chapter 22: 2018–2020: Tariff Tantrums to the Pandemic

## Core Lessons

1. *"Have a hard invalidation level (previous correction low) and exit all bullish bets below it."*

2. *"Extreme oversold conditions in Bank Nifty mean short covering can add ~300 Nifty points instantly."*

3. *"Consolidation after vertical rallies is violent, not quiet: expect multi-thousand-point intraday swings with flat closes."*

4. *"Option sellers can pre-commit to an expiry range from max-OI strikes and hedge the tails."*

5. *"Sell puts on intraday falls in a range with supportive OI; skip the call side when upside is capped."*

6. *"Falling VIX (30->25) with 200-point weekly ranges = consolidation before expansion."*

7. *"Previous day's average traded price (VWAP) acts as intraday support/resistance."*

8. *"Eroding futures premium versus spot warns that a runaway rally is turning into consolidation."*

9. *"Mid/small caps rallying 5-10% while large caps consolidate signals underlying bullish breadth."*

10. *"Event clarity releases sidelined money - the rally after resolution often exceeds the pre-event drift."*

11. *"Max-OI put strikes show option sellers' conviction levels about support."*

12. *"A single mega-news (Jio-Facebook) can lift the whole index via its heavyweight."*


## Key Takeaways

- Have a hard invalidation level (previous correction low) and exit all bullish bets below it.
- Extreme oversold conditions in Bank Nifty mean short covering can add ~300 Nifty points instantly.
- Consolidation after vertical rallies is violent, not quiet: expect multi-thousand-point intraday swings with flat closes.
- Option sellers can pre-commit to an expiry range from max-OI strikes and hedge the tails.
- Sell puts on intraday falls in a range with supportive OI; skip the call side when upside is capped.
- Falling VIX (30->25) with 200-point weekly ranges = consolidation before expansion.
