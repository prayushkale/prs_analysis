# Chapter 13: The Seller's Edge: Short Straddles & Strangles

## Core Lessons

1. *"No strategy works in all conditions - the adjustment process is the real skill."*

2. *"Backtests that ignore charges and slippage overstate returns; real costs can consume most of a small account's capital."*

3. *"In violent moves, premiums behave abnormally because losing-side sellers are forced to square up and roll - not because the market 'should' behave that way."*

4. *"Stock options demand institutional-scale margin - one Adani straddle lot ~5 lakh; deploy only ~25% of capital and keep the rest for adjustments."*

5. *"Short strangle is called the most dangerous strategy - unlimited one-sided loss with both legs naked."*

6. *"Crypto ATM straddle premiums run multiples of index premiums at comparable underlying levels - richer premium for richer (but gap-free) risk."*

7. *"No strategy is self-sufficient: a wrong directional view was rescued entirely by layered adjustments (multiple straddle strikes, staggered entries)."*

8. *"Stamp duty is paid on both sides; with STT, brokerage and GST, costs compound daily - annualize them before believing any strategy."*

9. *"No mechanical system survives unadjusted: if one did, it would be arbitraged away - adjustment and position movement are the actual skill."*

10. *"You make small small profits - that is how option sellers survive."*

11. *"Buyback news pops are sentiment, not value: capped tender sizes make the arithmetic trivial - fade the euphoria, don't chase it."*

12. *"Portfolio margin benefit accrues to hedged positions but only if opened after the feature existed."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Strangle roll-under-stress mechanics

**Construction:** Short 9,000CE + 7,000PE written Friday around spot 8,000; on a violent rally, buy back 7,000PE (releasing margin) and sell 7,500/8,000PE instead

**Numbers:** Legs 1,000 points either side of spot; roll targets 500-1,000 points closer

**Best used when:** After violent directional moves when the losing leg must be repositioned

**Adjustments:** Roll the losing short leg closer using either spare cash (preferred) or margin released by squaring the far leg

**Risks:** Forced buyback at inflated premiums; whipsaw if the market reverses immediately


### Unhedged short straddle on a single stock

**Construction:** Sell 1000 CE (49) + sell 1000 PE (39) on HDFC Bank, 2 lots (1000 qty), June 9 2020, hold to near expiry

**Numbers:** Premium collected ~87,000; returned ~35,000; profit ~50,000; stock closed 1035 on June 25; daily combined premium stayed below 87 through expiry

**Best used when:** High IV with range-bound expectation around a support level (1000)

**Adjustments:** None taken in this trade - held naked; would need firefighting if spot ran beyond breakevens

**Risks:** Unlimited loss beyond 1087/913; a single-stock gap through breakevens is the failure mode


### Shifting short straddle (Bank Nifty)

**Construction:** Sell ATM straddle monthly (21500 @2400 combined); shift to 22000, then 22500 as market trends; run weekly ATM reference trades alongside

**Numbers:** ~3.5 lakh straddle profit + ~1.76 lakh weekly profits ≈ 8 lakh on ~1 crore capital in ~2 weeks (July 2020)

**Best used when:** Trending or wide-range months where the index keeps moving away from the initial ATM

**Adjustments:** Shift straddle up/down on sustained moves; close weekly legs before direction flips

**Risks:** Whipsaw periods cause repeated shifts with slippage; uncapped loss if a shift is missed


### Put ratio spread with support defense (Nifty)

**Construction:** Buy 10500 PE (750 qty @430); sell 2x 10000 PE, 2x 9500 PE, 1x 9000 PE; lower breakeven 9500; add a second round of the same short puts after a rally

**Numbers:** Long put -1,84,000; short legs +2,59,000 +45,000 +1,20,000; base trade +2,41,000; with weeklies ~4,23,000

**Best used when:** Defended support below market; mildly bullish-to-neutral

**Adjustments:** Re-sell the short-put ladder after premium decay; keep the long put as disaster hedge

**Risks:** Break below support with doubled short puts = accelerated loss; margin-heavy


### Naked ITM put + far call reference (Bank Nifty)

**Construction:** Sell 22000 PE (ITM, @1500, 900 qty) with Bank Nifty at 21500; sell 25000 CE as reference; weekly trades alongside

**Numbers:** Capital ~80 lakh; target ~10 lakh for the series

**Best used when:** Strong bullish bias with rich ITM premium

**Adjustments:** Reference call sold as upside hedge; weekly scalps

**Risks:** Naked ITM put loses point-for-point below strike; no long put protection

- Stock options demand institutional-scale margin - one Adani straddle lot ~5 lakh; deploy only ~25% of capital and keep the rest for adjustments.
- Short strangle is called the most dangerous strategy - unlimited one-sided loss with both legs naked.
- Crypto ATM straddle premiums run multiples of index premiums at comparable underlying levels - richer premium for richer (but gap-free) risk.
