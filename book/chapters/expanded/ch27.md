# Chapter 27: The Regulator's Dilemma

## Core Lessons

1. *"Intraday margin scales with stock volatility: roughly 4.5x leverage on Reliance (~22%) but barely 1.3x on Idea (~77.5%) at the time."*

2. *"Indian promoters would exploit a squeeze by issuing shares at inflated prices - a structural anti-squeeze valve."*

3. *"Central banks now manage markets both ways: hawkish talk to cap rallies, dovish rescue at lows - trade the zigzag, not the narrative."*

4. *"Stocks below book value with improving balance sheets offer re-rating upside with limited downside."*

5. *"India's F&O-to-cash turnover ratio is the world's highest - the regulatory trigger for margin tightening."*

6. *"From August 2020, selling shares you already hold also requires margin in the account - the exchange cannot tell an intraday sell from a delivery sell."*

7. *"Low rates push spending and asset prices up; hikes redirect money to deposits and cool demand."*

8. *"Settlement mechanics: T-day trade, T+1 funds/shares to broker/exchange, T+2 demat/money transfer; early pay-in avoids the shortfall but forfeits the auction-market rescue for short deliveries."*

9. *"Pledged securities remain yours; only default triggers liquidation."*

10. *"Regulators should enable business growth; some rules (extra margin on long-dated options) backfire and shrink liquidity."*

11. *"When F&O interest goes down, cash market interest also goes down - they are linked"*

12. *"Brokers can exploit the ~5-session reporting window; clients who know the rules recover wrongly charged penalties."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### BTC short strangle (live demo)

**Construction:** Sold BTC 68,000 call (~711 USDT, 100 contracts = 0.1 BTC, 25x leverage, margin ~273 USDT) and sold BTC 36,000 put (~255 USDT margin) for late-January expiry

**Numbers:** Expecting BTC to stay between 36,000 and 68,000 dollars; combined margin ~528 USDT

**Best used when:** Range-bound high-IV crypto; one-month horizon

**Adjustments:** Exchange-native take-profit and stop-loss settings due to 24-hour trading

**Risks:** Full margin loss on a breach of either breakeven; exchange/counterparty risk; 0.05% fee drag


### PSU bank value + ETF entry

**Construction:** PSU Bank BeES ETF (or PSU equity fund); SIP with added buying on 5-10% corrections

**Numbers:** PSU bank index +20% (Nov 21-Nov 22) and +32% (Nov 21-Nov 18, 2022) vs Nifty +1.2%/+2%; private banks +2.8%; Bank of Baroda P/B 0.93 vs HDFC 3.6x, ICICI 3.5x, Kotak 4x; speaker's own ETF position ~+40%

**Best used when:** Early re-rating phase after cleanup; low P/B with improving profitability

**Adjustments:** Add on dips rather than chase; expect low dividends from PSU banks (unlike PFC/REC/Coal India)

**Risks:** Sector concentration; entry near market all-time highs; re-rating may stall


### Defensive-sector allocation at rich valuations

**Construction:** Prefer IT, pharma, FMCG over cyclicals when valuations are stretched

**Numbers:** All three defensive sectors up >10% in the prior month per speaker

**Best used when:** Overbought, rich-valuation markets for investors who must stay invested

**Adjustments:** Switch to cyclicals when rate cycle turns and valuations normalize

**Risks:** Defensives can still correct with the whole market


## Key Takeaways

- Intraday margin scales with stock volatility: roughly 4.5x leverage on Reliance (~22%) but barely 1.3x on Idea (~77.5%) at the time.
- Indian promoters would exploit a squeeze by issuing shares at inflated prices - a structural anti-squeeze valve.
- Central banks now manage markets both ways: hawkish talk to cap rallies, dovish rescue at lows - trade the zigzag, not the narrative.
- Stocks below book value with improving balance sheets offer re-rating upside with limited downside.
- India's F&O-to-cash turnover ratio is the world's highest - the regulatory trigger for margin tightening.
- From August 2020, selling shares you already hold also requires margin in the account - the exchange cannot tell an intraday sell from a delivery sell.
