## Strategy Spotlight

### BTC financed bearish combo (Oct 2022 expiry)

**Construction:** Buy 1x 22,000 put (~$3,600); sell 4x 15,000 put (~$600 each), 13,000 put (~$328), 25,000 call (~$540), 30,000 call (~$167); 20x leverage, size 1,000/4,000 quantities

**Numbers:** Margin ~$11,000; lower breakeven ~$12,800; no-loss up to 25,000; max profit ~$7,000 near 15,000-16,000; ~15% on margin in ~2.5 months (~6%/month)

**Best used when:** Range-bound to mildly falling market after a technical bounce you expect to fade

**Adjustments:** Prior month's ETH 1,600 straddle: squared the put ahead of CPI/Fed, re-sold 1,200 puts 4x; netted ~$1,200 on ~$2,200 margin (40-50%)

**Risks:** Loss begins above 25,000; slippage on crypto options is heavy at market orders; unregulated market, no SEBI recourse

### Trend-following allocation mix

**Construction:** Split capital ~4 ways: two non-directional (straddle-family) strategies + two directional (trend-following) strategies of different horizons

**Numbers:** Conceptual allocation: roughly 20-25% per method

**Best used when:** All regimes - the mix, not the signal, is the risk control

**Adjustments:** Rebalance emphasis as directional trends pay or non-directional premiums pay

**Risks:** Directional books suffer in violent news-driven reversals; non-directional suffers in sustained trends
