## Core Lessons

1. *"VIX at multi-year lows caps daily range: 50-100 point Nifty days, premiums decay both sides - sell time, don't buy direction"*

2. *"When VIX falls while price falls, downside is limited"*

3. *"The real systemic risk is DII participation stopping - FII selling alone is absorbable."*

4. *"Markets make bigger moves just before or just after major events; the drift often continues both sides of the event."*

5. *"Falling VIX on volatile closes keeps the premium-selling edge alive."*

6. *"Breadth divergence: a handful of heavyweights can mask a bear market underneath a record index."*

7. *"Each failed test weakens resistance until it breaks."*

8. *"Bull-market corrections and bear-market relief rallies are both fast and furious - 1,100 up in a week, 950 down in two."*

9. *"Never predict the day-after-results move - positioning, not the print, sets it."*

10. *"Two index stocks can account for an entire index move - concentration risk"*

11. *"Run-off elections convert one-day event risk into a month of uncertainty - uncertainty, not direction, is what markets cannot price."*

12. *"Offset feared delivery: a losing put can be neutralized by selling calls higher up (his LIC hedge ladder)."*


## Key Takeaways

- VIX at multi-year lows caps daily range: 50-100 point Nifty days, premiums decay both sides - sell time, don't buy direction
- When VIX falls while price falls, downside is limited
- The real systemic risk is DII participation stopping - FII selling alone is absorbable.
- Markets make bigger moves just before or just after major events; the drift often continues both sides of the event.
- Falling VIX on volatile closes keeps the premium-selling edge alive.
- Breadth divergence: a handful of heavyweights can mask a bear market underneath a record index.
