# Chapter 3: Discipline, Process, and Surviving First

## Core Lessons

1. *"Large positions in single stocks (a trapped high-volume hedge) can cost crores in one month - size is destiny."*

2. *"Index contribution equals price move times free-float weight; read it to know what actually moved the index."*

3. *"Only bring to the market what you can afford to lose entirely."*

4. *"Yield curve inversion (short-term yield above long-term) has preceded every modern US recession - a macro red flag, not a timing tool."*

5. *"Public-domain news is already priced in; without a genuine informational edge you cannot profit from it."*

6. *"Derivatives are a negative-sum game: both winner and loser pay brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange charges and taxes (~Rs 50,000 cr/yr drained; STT alone ~Rs 6,000 cr)."*

7. *"Margin benefit applies only after both hedged legs reach the backend: square off from open positions, or fresh counter-orders demand full margin."*

8. *"Option sellers receive premium in cash while posting margin via collateral, so the premium itself can earn interest - a structural edge over option buyers."*

9. *"Speculative manias (tulips) taught markets the need for hedging instruments."*

10. *"Simple, executable solutions usually beat sophisticated ones - complexity is often performance theater."*

11. *"Corporate loans from directors are capital, not debt — a common misread of MCA filings."*

12. *"Check RMS limits before every add: cash margin available minus margin used = real firing power."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### Multi-strike, multi-expiry put selling (April 2020)

**Construction:** Sold put options spread across various strike prices and expiry periods rather than one strike

**Numbers:** 7500PE sold around Rs.75 during a morning dip; SGX Nifty ~9200 vs ~9000 close

**Best used when:** Ultra-bearish consensus with the negative surprise already announced

**Adjustments:** Exited or rolled by expiry; profit disclosed in a later review video

**Risks:** Losses if the market breaks below the sold strikes; premium is the cap on profit


### Monthly short option + weekly protective hedge

**Construction:** Sell far-month option; buy near-week option slightly OTM below breakeven; re-buy weekly

**Numbers:** 9,000PE July sold (>100 premium), 8,900PE weekly bought; margin ~50,000 vs >1,00,000 unhedged

**Best used when:** credit selling with black-swan protection

**Adjustments:** roll weekly wing; broker far-strike restrictions are the friction

**Risks:** weekly wing expires worthless after rallies; rollover cost creep


### TCS earnings combo (put sale + call ratio with financed long call)

**Construction:** Sell 2 lots 2000 PE; buy 2 lots 2300 CE @47.75; sell 4 lots 2400 CE; sell 1 lot 2500 CE

**Numbers:** Margin 3,65,000; breakevens 1988-2508; probability of profit ~82%; max profit ~65,000; result after 5:30 pm, reaction next session

**Best used when:** Pre-earnings IV elevation on a low-beta large cap expected to move modestly

**Adjustments:** Stop-loss or fire-fight if stock breaches wings; exit after IV crush realizes

**Risks:** Gap beyond 2508 on exceptional results; assignment/liquidity on stock options


### Nifty long / Bank Nifty short pair

**Construction:** Long 1 lot Nifty futures + short 1 lot Bank Nifty futures (25 lot size), rebalanced views weekly

**Numbers:** One-week window: ~4,725 loss on Nifty leg vs ~12,775 profit on Bank Nifty leg; two-week window: net ~12,000+ profit; one-month window positive

**Best used when:** Index divergence phases - Bank Nifty underperforming Nifty for weeks

**Adjustments:** Re-evaluate pair weekly; exit when the spread normalizes

**Risks:** Correlation breakdown in crashes; both legs can lose if sized wrong; margin on two futures legs


### IPO cost-recovery hold

**Construction:** Apply to quality IPOs with idle funds parked in 6-6.5% savings accounts; on allotment sell partial quantity to recover application cost; hold remainder free

**Numbers:** Example: L&T issue ~1987-88, partly convertible debentures Rs50 doubled to ~Rs100 next day; held value estimated Rs50-60L today

**Best used when:** Small capital, no F&O skill yet; strong primary-market pipelines

**Adjustments:** Review holdings annually; keep proceeds cycling into fresh quality IPOs

**Risks:** Opportunity cost if markets run; allotment not guaranteed (refund is safe)
