# Chapter 6: Reading the Option Chain

## Core Lessons

1. *"Check MWPL utilization before entering any mid/small-cap F&O position; the NSE publishes next-day ban lists and combined OI daily."*

2. *"Breakeven at expiry = strike plus premium for calls, strike minus premium for puts; profit territory begins only beyond breakeven."*

3. *"Volume measures activity and liquidity; open interest measures committed overnight risk."*

4. *"In downtrends good news rallies in specific stocks get sold."*

5. *"Probability of profit for a typical slightly-OTM long option is around 30% - buyers lose most of the time by design."*

6. *"The same day's volume can hide opposite outcomes: square-offs shrink OI, fresh two-sided trades grow it."*

7. *"Short-covering rallies lack a fundamental cause - global cues keep worsening while price rises."*

8. *"Exit polls have been badly wrong (Trump 2016, Brexit, Chhattisgarh 2023) — never size positions as if they are results."*

9. *"Monthly expected range = ATM strike +/- the ATM straddle premium; roughly 65% of months stay inside it."*

10. *"Price up + OI up = long build-up; price up + OI down = short covering; price down + OI up = short build-up; price down + OI down = long unwinding."*

11. *"Change in OI matters more than its level - most option-chain volume is intraday play, not committed positions."*

12. *"The price/OI matrix is a guideline, not a golden rule."*


## Strategy Spotlight

### ATM straddle range projection

**Construction:** Nearest strike to post-expiry close; add ATM call + put premiums to strike for upper bound, subtract for lower bound

**Numbers:** Nifty 11,650 strike; call 220 + put 190 = 410; range 11,240-12,060 (rounded 11,200-12,100); ~65% probability

**Best used when:** Start of a monthly series, day after expiry

**Adjustments:** Update if a large event (election/budget) reprices premiums mid-month

**Risks:** Fat-tail months with news shocks breach the range; not a certainty


### Long call (worked example)

**Construction:** Buy 1 lot Nifty 13,200CE (lot 75) at 170

**Numbers:** premium Rs 12,750; breakeven 13,370; max loss Rs 12,750; ~29% POP

**Best used when:** strong directional view with time for the move

**Adjustments:** square up any time as premium tracks spot

**Risks:** total premium loss if spot below strike at expiry


### Long put (worked example)

**Construction:** Buy 1 lot Nifty 12,800PE (lot 75) at 186

**Numbers:** premium Rs 13,950; breakeven 12,614; max loss = premium

**Best used when:** bearish view or crash protection

**Adjustments:** exit anytime

**Risks:** total premium loss if spot above strike at expiry


### Intraday fade-the-bounce rule

**Construction:** Trend down: short 100-125 pts above intraday low; trend up: long 100-125 pts below intraday high

**Numbers:** Nifty bounced ~120 pts then fell ~60 pts same day

**Best used when:** established trend, no fresh catalyst

**Adjustments:** exit if trend flips; respect stop

**Risks:** fails on trend-change days; news-driven reversals


### Election result collar (portfolio hedge)

**Construction:** Buy June 23,000 put (~₹450-500) + sell 25,000/26,000 call to finance it

**Numbers:** Put premium inflated ~300→450-600 by event IV; net cost near zero

**Best used when:** Portfolio protection into binary events at range highs

**Adjustments:** Re-hedge on the post-gap-up euphoric morning if missed earlier

**Risks:** Upside capped at call strike; portfolio bleeds between strikes; premium decay if event passes quietly
