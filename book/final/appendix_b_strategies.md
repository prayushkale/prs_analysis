## Appendix B: Strategy Quick Reference

| Strategy | Construction | Best regime | Principal risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collateral-funded option selling (framework) | Sell options against collateral: bank FD, shares, mutual funds or bank guarantee pledged to the broker | Traders with large capital seeking steady returns | Margin calls on booked losses; losses can exceed premium received if unhedged |
| Falling-VIX combo (call ratio + put ratio) | Bought 8,200CE, sold 3x 9,000CE; bought 8,000PE, sold 7,400PE + 7,300PE (as stated) | Falling VIX with a directional view; benefits from vega collapse on the short legs | Upside loss above 9,000 on the 3:1 call ratio; downside acceleration through 7,300 on the put ratio |
| Strangle roll-under-stress mechanics | Short 9,000CE + 7,000PE written Friday around spot 8,000; on a violent rally, buy back 7,000PE (releasing margin) and sell 7,500/8,000PE instead | After violent directional moves when the losing leg must be repositioned | Forced buyback at inflated premiums; whipsaw if the market reverses immediately |
| Strike-and-expiry put ladder (illustrative, high risk) | Sold puts at 6,500 / 7,000 / 7,300 / 7,400 / 7,500 / 7,800 / 8,500 across weekly, May and June expiries; small stock-option positions (IndusInd 800 qty @22.52; ~2,300PE on a NBFC s | Extreme high VIX with stabilizing market (COVID April 2020) | Aggressive directional sizing; a renewed crash through the ladder strikes would produce large losses; explicitly not replicable by retail |
| Multi-strike, multi-expiry put selling (April 2020) | Sold put options spread across various strike prices and expiry periods rather than one strike | Ultra-bearish consensus with the negative surprise already announced | Losses if the market breaks below the sold strikes; premium is the cap on profit |
| Cash Secured Put | Sell a put at the strike where you want to own the stock; keep the full cash ready for delivery | Sideways-to-up markets, or when you want a lower entry price in a quality stock | Profit capped at premium; stock can fall far below strike; requires full cash backing of the strike |
| Infosys earnings iron fly with financed wings | Sell 650CE + 650PE (5 lots); buy 720CE + 580PE; sell 2x 750CE (10 lots) and 2x 550PE to finance the wings | Results eve with elevated implied volatility | Beyond 750/550 the financing legs turn the risk unlimited again; overnight gap risk |
| IndusInd Bank earnings hedged straddle (May series) | Sell May 400CE @62 + 400PE @52; buy current-month 500CE @4.25 + 300PE @5.25 as protection | High-IV stock ahead of results; next-month premium rich relative to risk | Very large move beyond the wings; one month is a long time in trading |
| IndusInd earnings-day iron-fly style combo | Sell 400CE + sell 400PE, buy 500CE + buy 300PE (current month) | earnings day when heavy short positioning built up | big gap moves on expiry; adjustments need reserve capital |
| Put-selling ladder in collapsing-VIX rebound | Sold puts across strikes from ~6,100 to 9,000 during April 2020 recovery | post-crash VIX collapse with market refusing to make new lows | windfall not repeatable; VIX normalizes; requires conviction view |
| ICICI Bank call ratio (1x2) for earnings | Buy 1 lot 350CE at ~2,440; sell 2 lots 380CE at ~1,190 | mildly bullish stock pinned below known resistance into results | gap above the sold strike doubles exposure; earnings gaps both ways |
| OTM credit-sale adjustment on a losing ratio | Existing: long 350CE, short 2x 380CE (max loss ~0.50/share ~ Rs 800/lot). Adjustment: sell 1x 400CE at ~3.65 | After a results miss confirms the directional view is dead | New short call adds upside risk; acceptable because view is now neutral-bearish |
| Staggered index investing with cash-secured puts | SIP into a Nifty 50 ETF; optionally place cash-secured puts at desired lower entries | Post-crash pessimism; long horizon | Multi-year drawdowns; requires no-liquidity-needs capital |
| Futures-plus-collateral long-term long | Buy 1 lot long futures; park notional value in FD/Bharat Bond; pledge for collateral margin | Large capital, long-term bullish, willing to manage monthly rollovers | Monthly rollover discipline; gap/MTM risk on the futures leg; lot-size minimum locks ~7.5L capital |
| Cash-secured puts (ICICI 300PE, HDFC 840PE) | Sell puts below market with full cash set aside for delivery | moderately bullish with stocks near support | sharp breakdown below strike forces delivery at loss |
| Hedged short option (spread) | Sell 10,000C / buy 10,200C (200pt); variants 300-400pt | any directional credit view | max loss = width x lot; premium give-up |
| Liquid BeES cash-component collateral | Buy Liquid BeES; pledge as the cash component of margin | any time idle capital sits in a trading account | haircut 10%; liquidity depth |
| Monthly short option + weekly protective hedge | Sell far-month option; buy near-week option slightly OTM below breakeven; re-buy weekly | credit selling with black-swan protection | weekly wing expires worthless after rallies; rollover cost creep |

| Hedged put sale (index) | Sell 1600 put, buy 1500 put (June expiry); Bank Nifty call ladder 21500/22000/22500/23000 with weekly hedges | High-VIX range with defined support | Range break below support |
| Limited-risk call ratio spread (1:3:2) | Buy 1x 10,500 CE (Aug), sell 3x 11,000 CE (Aug), buy 2x 11,500 CE (Aug) | After a sharp rally, into strong resistance (200-DMA + 50% retracement of 12,400->7,500) | Sustained breakout above 11,500 hits max loss; requires two-month view |
| Expiry-day naked call selling (documented failure) | Sell 10,000 CE on weekly expiry day (18 June 2020) in size at Rs 4 | High-probability decay days only, with strict size | Low-premium options have violent multi-fold spikes; this is the canonical blowup pattern |

| Collateral-funded option selling (HNI pattern) | Pledge debt mutual funds/bank FDs as collateral; sell options against the margin; keep interest as primary return | Wealthy, income-focused sellers who accept trading as a bonus stream | Trading losses eat the collateral cushion; interest is not guaranteed against credit events |
| TCS earnings combo (put sale + call ratio with financed long call) | Sell 2 lots 2000 PE; buy 2 lots 2300 CE @47.75; sell 4 lots 2400 CE; sell 1 lot 2500 CE | Pre-earnings IV elevation on a low-beta large cap expected to move modestly | Gap beyond 2508 on exceptional results; assignment/liquidity on stock options |
| Covered call via futures leg (margin-optimized) | Hold long futures instead of physical shares; sell calls against it | Large holders wanting option income without physical-settlement complexity | Futures MTM swings; margin calls if exposure margin rules change |
| Infosys earnings iron structure (put debit spread + call ratio credit wings) | Buy 800 PE (1 lot); sell 770 PE (1 lot); sell 830 CE (2 lots); buy 900 CE (2 lots) | Pre-earnings on a momentum stock near highs where a moderate move is likelier than a blowout | Strong beat-and-raise above 900 creates the defined max loss; low liquidity in stock options |
| Shifting short straddle (Bank Nifty) | Sell ATM straddle monthly (21500 @2400 combined); shift to 22000, then 22500 as market trends; run weekly ATM reference trades alongside | Trending or wide-range months where the index keeps moving away from the initial ATM | Whipsaw periods cause repeated shifts with slippage; uncapped loss if a shift is missed |
| FD/collateral-backed premium recycling | ~25 lakh FD pledged; premiums from option selling recycled to absorb small booked losses without touching the FD | Option sellers running multiple small premium streams | If booked losses exceed total premium received, cash injection is mandatory |
