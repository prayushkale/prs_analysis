## Strategy Spotlight

### BTC financed bearish combo (Oct 2022 expiry)

**Construction:** Buy 1x 22,000 put (~$3,600); sell 4x 15,000 put (~$600 each), 13,000 put (~$328), 25,000 call (~$540), 30,000 call (~$167); 20x leverage, size 1,000/4,000 quantities

**Numbers:** Margin ~$11,000; lower breakeven ~$12,800; no-loss up to 25,000; max profit ~$7,000 near 15,000-16,000; ~15% on margin in ~2.5 months (~6%/month)

**Best used when:** Range-bound to mildly falling market after a technical bounce you expect to fade

**Adjustments:** Prior month's ETH 1,600 straddle: squared the put ahead of CPI/Fed, re-sold 1,200 puts 4x; netted ~$1,200 on ~$2,200 margin (40-50%)

**Risks:** Loss begins above 25,000; slippage on crypto options is heavy at market orders; unregulated market, no SEBI recourse

### Trend-following allocation mix

**Construction:** Split capital ~4 ways: two non-directional (straddle-family) strategies + two directional (trend-following) strategies of different horizons

**Numbers:** Conceptual allocation: roughly 20-25% per method

**Best used when:** All regimes - the mix, not the signal, is the risk control

**Adjustments:** Rebalance emphasis as directional trends pay or non-directional premiums pay

**Risks:** Directional books suffer in violent news-driven reversals; non-directional suffers in sustained trends

# Appendix A: Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ATM | At The Money — strike closest to spot price |
| ITM | In The Money — option has intrinsic value |
| OTM | Out of The Money — option has only time value |
| IV | Implied Volatility — market's forecast of future volatility |
| VIX | India VIX — volatility index calculated from Nifty option prices |
| FII | Foreign Institutional Investor |
| DII | Domestic Institutional Investor |
| OI | Open Interest — total outstanding contracts |
| PCR | Put-Call Ratio — contrarian sentiment indicator |
| STT | Securities Transaction Tax — levied on every buy/sell |
| PE ratio | Price-to-Earnings ratio — valuation metric |
| Max OI | Strike with highest open interest — often acts as support/resistance |
| GIFT Nifty | Global Index for Financial Trade Nifty — Singapore-based Nifty futures (pre-2023: SGX Nifty) |
| LEAPS | Long-term Equity Anticipation Securities — options with expiry >1 year |
| Span margin | Initial margin based on worst-case loss scenario using SPAN methodology |
| Exposure margin | Additional margin during high volatility or near expiry |
| Breakeven | Strike ± premium paid/received — the price at which the trade breaks even |
| Theta | Time decay — how much option value erodes per day |
| Delta | Change in option price per 1-point move in underlying |
| Gamma | Rate of change of Delta per 1-point move |
| Vega | Sensitivity of option price to 1% change in IV |

# Appendix B: Strategy Quick-Reference

| Strategy | Construction | Best Regime | Max Risk |
|----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Short Straddle | Sell ATM Call + Sell ATM Put | Low VIX, range-bound market | Unlimited (both sides) |
| Short Strangle | Sell OTM Call + Sell OTM Put | Low VIX, expected range holds | Unlimited (both sides) |
| Bull Call Spread | Buy ATM Call + Sell OTM Call | Bullish, capped upside | Premium paid |
| Bear Put Spread | Buy ATM Put + Sell OTM Put | Bearish, capped downside | Premium paid |
| Call Calendar | Sell near-month Call + Buy far-month Call | Consolidation after big move | Time decay loss on far leg |
| Put Calendar | Sell near-month Put + Buy far-month Put | Consolidation after big move | Time decay loss on far leg |
| Call Ratio Spread | Buy 1 OTM Call + Sell 2+ further OTM Calls | Mildly bullish, downside neutral | Unlimited on extreme rally |
| Put Ratio Spread | Buy 1 OTM Put + Sell 2+ further OTM Puts | Mildly bearish, upside neutral | Unlimited on extreme fall |
| Call Butterfly | Buy 1 lower strike + Sell 2 middle + Buy 1 higher strike | Range-bound after earnings/events | Premium paid (limited) |
| Put Butterfly | Same construction in puts | Range-bound, bearish bias | Premium paid (limited) |
| BeES + Protective Put | Buy Nifty BeES + Buy far-dated OTM Put | Long-term bullish with tail risk | Premium of put + exit at support |
| Covered Call | Own 100 shares + Sell 1 OTM Call | Long stock, mild upside expected | Stock holds downside uncapped |
| Cash-Secured Put | Assign cash + Sell 1 OTM Put at strike you'd buy | Want to buy stock at discount | Stock falls below strike |
