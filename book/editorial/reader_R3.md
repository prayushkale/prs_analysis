## Voice & style notes

- Fixed weekly template, almost liturgical: (1) sponsor greeting, (2) last week's review vs own forecast, (3) global cues + SGX Nifty, (4) FII/DII flows with crores and long/short %, (5) coming week's event/data calendar (results named stock-by-stock with days), (6) OI support/resistance, (7) range forecast + "gut feeling," (8) "Hope you enjoyed watching this video. Thank you for watching."
- Numbers are the spine: every claim is quantified (₹60,000 cr FII inflow, −4,000 pts in a month, 83%→61% shorts, 200-DMA at 16,900).
- Repeated stock-market aphorisms deployed as section-punctuating maxims: "climbs the wall of worries," "buy on rumour sell on news," "markets react only to surprises," "amateurs open the market, professionals close it."
- Self-referential honesty about errors: opens by scoring his own prior call ("I mentioned resistance at 8,500... it has decisively broken it") — builds trust via track-record admission.
- "Gut feeling" explicitly layered on top of data: OI says one range, gut says another; both are stated and the divergence acknowledged.
- Indian-English cadence: "very very" intensifiers, "only" as emphatic postposition ("it has been going on like this only"), rhetorical question-answer loops ("Why? Because...").
- Colloquial humor anchored in market characters: ITC as "the 8th wonder of the world" (8th heavyweight), "jack of all trades, master of none" self-deprecation.
- Personal stake disclosed casually mid-analysis ("I am under a little bit stress" on Bank Nifty longs) — lessons taught from inside live positions.
- Teaches through repetition of frameworks: the same OI/positioning/VIX method appears every week, so the viewer learns the checklist by osmosis.
- Truncated-week and holiday logistics always flagged early (four sessions, results calendar shifted).
