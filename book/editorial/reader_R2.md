## Voice & style notes

- Conversational monologue built on chain-of-reasoning: global cues → GIFT Nifty → open → OI/premiums → levels → strategy, signposted "Number one... second thing... third thing."
- Heavy verbal fillers and re-starts ("So, you know...", "as I always say", "coming back to our market") — strip for print; the reasoning spine is what survives.
- Rule-of-thumb pedagogy: almost every observation is compressed into a numeric heuristic (gap 40–50 pts, dip 80–100/250–300 pts, VIX/16, PCR 0.7 vs 1.5).
- Aphorisms as lesson anchors: "good news is bad news", "consensus is dangerous", "markets reward only the minority", "bull market climbs the wall of worry", "no support in a downtrend".
- Numerical storytelling: precise crore/point counts for flows and moves (₹20k cr, 1,200 pts, 600-pt intraday swing) — concrete numbers are the teaching currency.
- Predictions always given as ranges with explicit invalidation levels (technical triggers, straddle-implied bands), rarely bare targets.
- Personal anecdotes as credibility devices (channel hack, friend's put trade, prop-desk source, workshop chatter) — engaging but identity-laden; keep the lesson, drop the biography.
- Self-deprecating humor and asides ("don't ask me what is the capital") soften the instruction; occasional irritation at regulators/brokers.
- Repetition is intentional: the same framework (FII/DII, OI, Fibs, straddle ranges) re-derived daily — the book must state it once, then reference it.
- Sign-off ritual: "Hope you enjoyed watching this video. Thank you for watching." — pure video artifact.
