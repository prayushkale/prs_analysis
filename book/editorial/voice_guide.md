# Voice Guide — "Dhanraj" Book

## Book voice (Decision: distillation + first-person anecdotes)
- **Default register**: clear third-person teaching voice — "A short straddle profits when…", "The market rewards…". Concepts stated once, crisply; the source repeats rules 2–3× per video — the book states each once.
- **Anecdotes**: first-person "I" reserved for personal war-stories, trades, losses, and personal philosophy, attributed to Dhanraj. Never mix registers inside a paragraph.
- **Numbers-dense but honest**: exact strikes, flows, percentages retained where they teach; every hard fact verified (Phase 3) or hedged. Levels from a specific day are always dated ("On 11 May 2022, Nifty…") — never presented as evergreen advice.
- **Analogies are the signature**: keep the best (restaurant-vs-stop-loss, bus-and-pothole, kirana-store failure rates, arranged-marriage strategy search, insurance premium = hedging, waiting-for-the-bus corrections). Rewrite them cleanly; drop video framing ("as I showed on screen").
- **Tone**: direct, plain Indian-English accepted but lightly standardized for print; self-deprecating honesty kept ("option writers have always been wrong — that includes me"); no hero worship, no politics, no lifestyle content.
- **Teaching scaffolding to preserve**: convert monthly returns to annualized; benchmark against business returns; anonymous-character parables (taxi driver, villager, friend); "define risk first, size small, keep hedges real".
- **Chapter anatomy for concept chapters**: hook (a dated market moment) → first-principles build → worked numeric example → adjustment/firefighting logic → risk boundary → takeaway box.

## Prose normalization
- Source has comma-spliced run-ons (pre-2021) and ASR garbles; all prose is regenerated, not copied. Quotes ≤25 words only where distinctive.
- Recycled aphorisms (keep as recurring motifs, once per part): "Markets worry about the lead indicator, not the lag indicator"; "A bull market is born out of pessimism, grows on skepticism, matures on optimism, dies of euphoria"; "Amateurs open the market, professionals close it"; "There is no support in a downtrend, no resistance in an uptrend."

## Structural conventions
- Dates: "11 May 2022". Currency: ₹ with Indian units (crore/lakh) as spoken; US$ where the event is global.
- Each chapter ends with "Key Takeaways" box (3–6 bullets).
- Enhancement rule: any complex topic (Greeks, IV, margin netting, calendar/ratio/butterfly math, index rebalancing, crypto tax) gets a worked example + payoff table + a "common mistakes" note.
- Prediction hygiene: speaker's dated calls may appear only as illustrations of method with their outcome noted; the book never endorses point targets.
